Ranked top 5 upcoming catalysts (next ~2 weeks)

1) US government shutdown — congressional votes/continuing‑resolution outcome  
- Why: Whether Congress reopens the government will directly move markets (growth outlook, federal pay, confidence) and determine policy spillovers (flights, SNAP); Senate action is expected in the coming days–two weeks.  

2) FAA flight‑restriction enforcement / widening flight cancellations  
- Why: FAA orders and airport capacity cuts are already triggering hundreds of cancellations; further enforcement or expansion of cuts over the next week will hit airlines, travel bookings and consumer sentiment immediately.  

3) Court / Supreme Court action on SNAP / food‑aid funding  
- Why: Judges and the Supreme Court are actively weighing orders that would force full SNAP funding — a ruling or stay within days would change near‑term household support, political pressure on lawmakers and consumer spending dynamics.  

4) US export‑control/decision on AI chip sales to China (Nvidia/Blackwell report)  
- Why: Any formal U.S. decision to block or restrict sales of scaled‑down AI chips to China would be a major tech/geo‑trade shock for semiconductor valuations and US‑China tech ties; announcements or guidance could arrive within the next 1–2 weeks.  

5) Missing/pushed US jobs report & near‑term labour data disruption from shutdown  
- Why: The shutdown has already led to missed official jobs data; publication delays, alternative releases or revealed weakness in near‑term labour signals this week will influence Fed expectations and risk sentiment.  

(These are interlinked — the shutdown/SNAP/flight catalysts dominate both market and geopolitical risk over the next fortnight.)
