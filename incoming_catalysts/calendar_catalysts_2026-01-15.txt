1) Jan 22 — US final GDP & Core PCE (Core PCE Price Index QoQ Final / Core PCE YoY)  
- Determines the Fed policy outlook, drives US rates and USD moves, and sets the tone for global asset allocation.

2) Jan 19 — China GDP & activity data (GDP, IP, retail sales, FAI, unemployment)  
- Biggest real-time read on Chinese growth and demand that moves commodities, EM risk and signals Beijing's policy stance.

3) Jan 23 — BoJ interest rate decision & Quarterly Outlook Report  
- Any shift or signalling by the BoJ can trigger large JPY moves, global carry-flow adjustments and Asian FX/ bond volatility.

4) Jan 19 — Euro‑area CPI final & Core (EA CPI Final / Core Inflation Rate YoY Final)  
- Key input to ECB rate expectations and European sovereign spreads; influences EUR and euro‑area risk assets.

5) Jan 20 — China Loan Prime Rate fixes (1Y & 5Y LPR)  
- Shows whether Beijing is loosening or supporting credit conditions (incl. housing), with material implications for Chinese markets and global commodity/EM sentiment.
