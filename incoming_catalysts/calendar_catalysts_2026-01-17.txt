1) China monthly/quarterly macro bundle (19 Jan — GDP, industrial production, retail sales, fixed-asset investment, house prices, unemployment)
- China growth/inflation cycle confirmation will drive global risk sentiment, commodity prices and EM flows; large scope to surprise and alter global growth outlook.

2) US Core PCE / PCE final (22 Jan — Core PCE QoQ/YoY MoM final and related PCE series)
- Fed’s preferred inflation gauge; final readings will materially affect US rates expectations and risk assets.

3) BoJ interest rate decision & Quarterly Outlook Report (23 Jan)
- Any change or guidance from the BoJ can move JPY, global yields and carry trades given Japan’s central role in global funding markets.

4) Eurozone CPI final & core (19 Jan — headline and core CPI final)
- Final CPI determines ECB policy credibility and European bond/equity moves; small revisions can shift rate expectations.

5) ECB Monetary Policy Meeting Accounts (22 Jan)
- Details on policymakers’ views and voting dynamics can reshape ECB tightening bets and move EUR and core yields.
