1) US Fed Interest Rate Decision + projections & press conference (2025-12-10) — the Fed sets the global policy benchmark and its dot‑plot/projections and press conference will drive rates, USD and risk sentiment worldwide.  
2) US CPI (headline & core) (2025-12-18) — monthly inflation data directly re‑prices Fed expectations and will move bond yields, equities and FX.  
3) US Nonfarm Payrolls (and wages) (2025-12-16) — the labour release is the primary real‑time gauge of US demand and wage pressure, influencing Fed policy bets and market risk.  
4) ECB Interest Rate Decision & press conference (2025-12-18) — changes or guidance from the ECB will shift euro‑area yields, EUR and global rate differentials vs the Fed.  
5) BoE Interest Rate Decision & minutes (2025-12-18) — UK policy/voting outcome will affect GBP, gilt yields and European financial conditions, especially given dovish/hawkish surprises.
