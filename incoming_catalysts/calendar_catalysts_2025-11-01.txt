   - NFP is the highest-impact macro datapoint: it moves Fed expectations, USD, equities and Treasury yields globally.

2) 2025-11-06 — BoE Interest Rate Decision & Monetary Policy Report  
   - BoE decisions and the vote split materially affect GBP, UK/Eurozone rates and signal global central‑bank divergence.

   - RBA guidance (and any surprise) drives AUD, commodity/EM risk sentiment and has outsized regional market effects.

   - China inflation and producer prices are key for global commodity demand, EM export outlook and China growth/monetary policy signals.

   - The refunding sets U.S. debt supply and term premium expectations — a major driver for global yields and fixed‑income volatility.
