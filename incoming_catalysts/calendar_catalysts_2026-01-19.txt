1) US — Fed interest rate decision + press conference (28 Jan)  
- Sets global policy expectations, moves rates/FX/risk assets and re-anchors market pricing.

2) CN — Quarterly GDP + suite of activity data (GDP, IP, retail sales, FAI, unemployment) (19 Jan)

4) JP — BoJ interest rate decision + Quarterly Outlook Report (23 Jan)  
- Any shift or guidance affects JPY, global carry flows and JGB yields with outsized market impact.

5) CN — Loan Prime Rate decision (1y & 5y) (20 Jan)  
- Policy signalling for credit costs and property/credit support in China — key for domestic stability and regional markets.
