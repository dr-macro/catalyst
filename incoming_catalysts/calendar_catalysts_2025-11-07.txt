Ranked top 5 catalysts

1) US CPI (headline & core) — 2025-11-13  
- Primary inflation read that will reprice Fed expectations, US yields and the dollar, and thus global risk/EM flows.

2) US Nonfarm Payrolls (NFP) — 2025-11-07  
- Biggest near‑term labour‑market shock risk that moves Fed path bets, market volatility, and USD/liquidity immediately.

3) China activity bundle (IP, retail sales, FAI, unemployment + house prices) — 2025-11-14  
- Comprehensive growth snapshot for the world’s second‑largest economy that drives commodity demand, EM sentiment and China‑sensitive equity sectors.

4) China CPI & PPI — 2025-11-09  
- Inflation/PPI prints that signal domestic demand strength/deflationary pressure and the odds of Chinese policy stimulus or easing.

5) US 10‑year note auction (and associated US Treasury supply schedule) — 2025-11-12 (10‑year) / 2025-11-13 (30‑year)  
- Large sovereign issuance can move the US yield curve, funding costs and cross‑asset positioning ahead of the big US data prints.
