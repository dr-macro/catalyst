4) Trump’s call for Fannie/Freddie to buy $200B of mortgage bonds (and related policy steps) — announced now; agency push/implementation status to unfold over days–weeks
- Why: If acted on or even credibly pursued, it influences mortgage-backed security demand, mortgage rates and housing stocks; but execution faces legal/operational hurdles so market reaction will be about credibility and agency responses.
