Ranked top 5 upcoming catalysts (next 2 weeks)

1) Fed interest-rate decision + Powell press conference (Jan 28–29)
- Why: Immediate market driver for rates, equities, bond yields and the dollar; Powell’s tone will shape risk appetite and policy expectations.

2) U.S. DHS‑funding impasse / looming partial government shutdown (days–two weeks)
- Why: A shutdown or high-risk funding standoff would hit confidence, disrupt services, raise fiscal uncertainty and move Treasury yields and safe havens.

3) Medicare Advantage payment proposal and insurer fallout (ongoing; regulatory/legislative moves in coming weeks)
- Why: The administration/CMS proposal to hold MA rates has already wiped ~ $100bn of insurer market value — final rule or Congressional action could materially reshape health‑insurer earnings and market breadth.

4) Dollar slide after Trump remarks and potential FX volatility/intervention talk (immediate; could evolve over days)
- Why: Rapid dollar weakness has sent gold/silver to records and strains EM currencies — further political comments or policy responses (or talk of intervention) could amplify global FX and capital‑flow moves.

5) Major tech layoffs (Amazon ≈16,000) and related corporate cost cuts (announced late Jan; rollout over weeks)
- Why: Large-scale layoffs at Amazon and other tech firms will matter for sector sentiment, consumer spending expectations and labor markets — market reaction and follow‑on announcements are likely in the short term.

(Notes: other important items — Micron’s big Singapore investment and chip/AI supply developments (ASML/Nvidia H200 approvals) — are consequential longer‑term catalysts but are either already partially priced or will play out beyond the immediate two‑week window.)
