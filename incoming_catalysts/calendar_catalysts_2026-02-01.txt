Ranked top 5 catalysts (next 2 weeks)

1) 2026-02-06 — US Nonfarm Payrolls (NFP)  
- Primary market mover for growth, Fed policy expectations, bond yields, USD and global risk sentiment.

2) 2026-02-11 — US CPI (headline & core)  
- Direct read on inflation and the Fed’s policy path; will drive bond yields, equities and FX.

3) 2026-02-05 — ECB Interest Rate Decision & Press Conference (Lagarde)  
- Eurozone policy/guidance can shift euro, European bond curves and spill over to global rates and bank sector risk.

4) 2026-02-05 — BoE Interest Rate Decision & Monetary Policy Report  
- Gilt market and sterling sensitivity to any surprise or message on UK inflation, growth and bank stress/financial plumbing.

5) 2026-02-04 (and 2026-02-02) — US Treasury Refunding Announcement (and Refunding Estimates)  
- Size/timing of Treasury issuance materially affects global duration supply, liquidity and term premium across markets.

(Admittedly many other items—ISM/PMIs, RBA decision, JGB auctions, BoJ minutes—could move regional markets, but the five above are most likely to have the largest cross‑market and geopolitical impact.)
