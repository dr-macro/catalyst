Top 5 upcoming catalysts by importance:

1) US Fed Interest Rate Decision + FOMC projections & press conference (2025-12-10)  
- Fed policy, dot plot and Powell’s tone are the single biggest drivers of global rates, FX and risk appetite.

2) US CPI (headline & core) (2025-12-18)  
- Monthly inflation data directly shapes Fed outlook and near‑term market pricing of rates, real yields and equities.

3) US Nonfarm Payrolls + wages (2025-12-16)  
- Payrolls and hourly earnings are the key real‑time macro read on US labour conditions that move Fed expectations and risk assets.

4) China big macro suite (House prices, IP, Retail Sales, FAI, Unemployment) (2025-12-15)  
- China growth data materially affect global commodity demand, EM sentiment and geopolitically sensitive trade flows.

5) ECB interest rate decision + press conference (2025-12-18)  
- ECB guidance and the deposit rate decision drive European bond markets, EUR direction and spillovers to global financial conditions.
