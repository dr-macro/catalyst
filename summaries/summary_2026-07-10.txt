  - Oil rose >$1/bbl on US strikes; US diesel futures posted biggest daily gains in four years

  - Germany to borrow €800bn for rearmament  
  - Nato rearmament drive sustaining ~195,000 US defence jobs  
  - Germany agrees to buy US Tomahawk missiles; Ukraine creating long‑range command and escalating strikes

4) Memory‑chip and AI‑hardware capital flows are outsized, producing mega equity issuance and capex commitments that are re‑rating markets.

  - Fed officials flagged risks warranting higher rates; market pricing shows ~50% odds of a 2026 hike on some prediction markets  
  - Commentary: resumed steepening impulses and warnings to “wait for yield spikes” in 10‑year Treasuries

6) Credit intermediation and private‑credit markets are seeing increased caution and restructuring activity.  
  - HSBC pulls back from riskier private credit lending  
  - Blackstone and TPG seek >$4bn for Hologic surgical unit; UBS implicated in exodus from Blue Owl private credit fund  
  - EU regulators struggle to access private credit data

7) Trade, procurement and industrial policy are shifting toward on‑shoring and “buy local” protection measures.  
  - EU drafts ‘Buy European’ public‑tender rules and electrification plan  
  - Japan urges giant pension fund to invest more domestically; Japan encouraging pension repatriation  
  - Canada not ready for UAE C$70bn investment; US eases some exports to UAE (military items, AI chips, satellites)

8) Geopolitical unpredictability (US‑Iran, Ukraine, NATO politics) is intermittently dominating market risk premia and commodity flows.  
  - US carries out strikes against Iran; Iran fires missiles; US‑Iran clashes push oil and gas prices and route disruptions  
  - NATO summit marked by US policy caprice; Trump threatens troop withdrawals and purges election agency members  
  - Russia bans diesel exports; Ukraine increasing strikes on Russian energy infrastructure

9) Climate extremes and grid/energy vulnerabilities are becoming immediate macro risks to growth and operations.  
  - Western Europe recorded its hottest June on record  
  - At least 12 killed in Spanish wildfire; Eurostar orders trains rated for 55°C summers  
  - Utility boss warns US faces blackouts from power supply shortfall

Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a higher‑volatility regime driven by converging shocks: an expensive AI infrastructure boom that lifts selective tech and chip flows, persistent inflation/real‑rate risk keeping bond volatility elevated, and recurring geopolitical/energy disruptions that intermittently reset risk premia and commodity prices. Positioning should balance asymmetric tail risks from energy/geopolitics with concentrated thematic exposure to AI hardware and defense rearmament.
