Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an AI‑driven structural re‑weighting: heavy capex and large debt issuance fund an infrastructure boom that is simultaneously disrupting software earnings and forcing sector rotation. Central banks sit near peak rates (markets split between a prolonged hold and later cuts), so data and geopolitics—especially Middle East, China supply‑chain moves and fiscal slippage—are producing episodic volatility across equities, bonds and commodities.
