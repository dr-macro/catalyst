Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a geopolitically driven, stagflation‑tilted risk‑off regime: an oil‑supply and shipping shock is pushing energy and defence sectors higher while safe havens (gold, dollar) benefit and equities—especially travel, banks and growth/AI‑exposed names—face rotation and volatility. Policy uncertainty is high: central banks must weigh inflationary supply shocks against slowing growth, complicating the timeline for rate cuts and market positioning.
