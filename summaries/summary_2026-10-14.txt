   - Discussion of 10‑year Treasury possibly reaching 6%

   - Scaleway to build AMD‑powered AI supercomputer in Sweden and an Nvidia‑powered site in Copenhagen

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑rates, higher‑volatility regime driven by rising real yields and tighter financial conditions, coinciding with elevated geopolitical risk that is lifting energy prices and prompting safe‑haven and flight‑to‑quality flows. Markets are rotating away from stretched AI/chip growth narratives into energy, value and yield plays while policy and regulatory uncertainty (fiscal deficits, AI oversight, election risks) add directional uncertainty.
