Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a late‑cycle, volatility‑prone regime: growth is slowing in China while Western economies show mixed labour/inflation signals, prompting active central‑bank manoeuvring and liquidity operations. Markets are reallocating away from richly priced AI/tech names into defensive assets, commodities and protection strategies as geopolitical and policy uncertainty rises.
