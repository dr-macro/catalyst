1) Fed pivot toward a more hawkish, less-communicative stance under Kevin Warsh that is repricing rates and risk.
- Fed held rates steady at June meeting but signalled a later hike
- Kevin Warsh skipped a rate-path ‘dot’ and launched a communications review
- Nearly half of Fed policymakers see a 2026 rate hike
- Traders now price a Fed hike by September; 2‑year Treasury yields spiked after the meeting

2) Treasury market stress and liquidity tightening as issuance and yields rise.
- Liquidity conditions beginning to tighten as Treasury borrowing accelerates (May 25)
- Treasury yields rose materially after Warsh’s debut meeting
- 2‑year Treasury yield described as “rocketing” higher in market coverage

3) US‑Iran interim deal reopened shipping and rapidly changed oil market dynamics from shock to potential glut.
- US and Iran signed a ceasefire / MoU to reopen the Strait of Hormuz (mid‑June)
- Three Saudi tankers carrying ~6 million barrels transited Hormuz after the deal
- Oil fell below $80 and to a 3½‑month / 3‑month low in subsequent trade
- IEA warns of a significant 2027 oil surplus; IEA says Middle East peace deal could herald oil glut next year
- Iran to get access to $6bn of frozen funds; deal reportedly tied to a $300bn fund (more than half already committed per one source)

4) AI regulatory shock centred on Anthropic is forcing market and policy reactions with cross‑border access curtailed.
- Trump administration froze Anthropic’s top AI models / restricted foreign use
- Anthropic halted access to top models and scrambled to restore access
- Anthropic facing litigation over limits on its $200/month plans
- G7 and US/Europe discussing access to cutting‑edge models; leaders pledge cooperation on AI access and oversight

5) SpaceX and related AI/tech M&A created a speculative surge and sizable capital transactions.
- SpaceX IPO valuation reported at $75bn (called world’s biggest IPO)
- SpaceX locked in a $60bn deal to buy Cursor (AI coding asset)
- SpaceX stock jumped ~50% in early trading then pulled back; options and bond issuance flows surged
- S&P gave SpaceX an investment‑grade (BBB) rating with stable outlook; bankers preparing potential $20bn bond offering

6) Global monetary tightening is broadening — from BOJ tightening to G10 rate‑hike expectations.
- Bank of Japan raised rates to 1% (first time since 1995)
- Bank of England held rates at 3.75% while assessing Gulf developments
- Coverage: “Rate hikes are on for the G10 economies”; many central banks signalling more restrictive policy paths
- Central banks repatriating gold and rethinking reserves amid rising geopolitical risk

7) Geopolitics remains elevated with defence procurement and munitions production accelerated.
- US invoked the Defense Production Act for munitions/supply chains
- UK to provide 150,000 drones to Ukraine as part of a $996m package
- GM and Lockheed Martin partnering to boost weapons production; Finland buying US glide bombs for F‑35s
- Russia/Ukraine strikes and large drone campaigns hitting energy and transport infrastructure

8) FX, commodities and safe‑haven flows: dollar strength and volatile precious‑metals and energy moves.
- Dollar extended gains to a two‑month / more‑than‑one‑year high on rate‑hike bets
- Gold dropped to a six‑month low after speculative selling, then firmed as geopolitics and Fed pauses were reassessed
- Oil swung sharply lower after Hormuz reopening; jet/jet‑fuel prices tumbled and gasoline fell under $4/gal in US reporting

9) Corporate upheaval: profit warnings, layoffs and sectoral repositioning amid AI and geopolitical shocks.
- BMW warned on profits; shares sank after a profit warning (China/Iran risks cited)
- Accenture shares fell ~14% after a weaker outlook tied to Iran war impact on clients
- Robinhood cutting ~10% of workforce; Rivian laying off hundreds
- Yum Brands selling Pizza Hut assets for $2.7bn
- JPMorgan blocked Anthropic access for Hong Kong staff

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
Monetary policy has shifted to a higher‑for‑longer, more data‑sensitive regime under a hawkish Fed chair and synchronised global tightening, lifting yields and the dollar while tightening liquidity. Geopolitical developments (US‑Iran accord, Ukraine conflict) are rapidly re‑shaping energy, defence and safe‑haven flows even as AI regulation and mega‑tech transactions drive sectoral volatility and capital reallocation.
