Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an AI‑driven risk‑on cycle concentrated in hyperscalers and select chip names, supported by heavy M&A and private capital, but with rising real yields, a strong dollar and elevated geopolitical/energy risks creating asymmetric tail risks; market breadth and valuations are narrow, so macro surprises (rates, oil, or geopolitics) could trigger sudden repricing.
