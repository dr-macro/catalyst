Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
We’re in an era of intense dispersion driven by AI: huge capex commitments to data centres versus a painful re‑rating of tech expectations, with the bond market forcing faster tightening of rate‑cut hopes. That backdrop—higher yields, geopolitical supply shocks and active policy moves on tariffs—favours selective cyclicals, defensive income plays and real assets (gold) while keeping volatility elevated across crypto and growth names.
