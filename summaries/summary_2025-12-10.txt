Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in an early easing regime: central banks have begun cutting (and signalling pauses), which has rotated flows back into risk assets and real assets (notably precious metals) while keeping volatility elevated around geopolitics and tech/AI policy. The market theme is a reallocation toward AI-capex winners and cyclical recovery plays, tempered by uneven global growth (China’s PPI weakness) and ongoing supply‑chain/security frictions.
