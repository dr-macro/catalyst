Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a high‑volatility, risk‑off regime driven by a large geopolitical shock that is elevating energy and insurance risk premia while pushing investors into duration and safe havens; concurrently, deep structural reallocation from AI is concentrating capital in chips, cloud and defence while triggering layoffs and regulatory clashes. Policymakers face a trade‑off between containing inflation and managing market/strategic instability, leaving markets sensitive to news and headline risk.
