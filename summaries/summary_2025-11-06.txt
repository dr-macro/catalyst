Current macro/markets regime — concise view:
The market is in an AI‑led, headline‑sensitive regime: equity leadership is concentrated in AI and data‑center beneficiaries while bond yields and central‑bank signaling drive style rotation. Policy and geopolitical shocks (shutdown, tariffs, China tech rules) create episodic volatility; investors are balancing strong tech earnings and big capital flows into AI against slowing pockets of consumer demand and rising corporate and labor costs.
