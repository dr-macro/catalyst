Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑longer, risk‑sensitive regime: bond yields have repriced sharply, driven by resilient employment and rate uncertainty, while geopolitical flare‑ups and energy tightness are intermittently pushing commodity inflation and risk premia higher. Simultaneously, concentrated AI investment and data‑centre buildouts are reshaping sectoral leadership but face financing, political and safety headwinds that increase dispersion and policy risk across markets.
