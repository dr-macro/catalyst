Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer rates regime with elevated volatility and wide sector dispersion: AI-driven capex demand is straining cloud/compute supply and credit, while geopolitics (Middle East, Russia/Ukraine, Starlink disputes) is keeping energy risk premia and safe‑haven flows high. Positioning needs to balance cyclicals tied to AI/data‑centre spending against defensives and credit‑quality differentiation.
