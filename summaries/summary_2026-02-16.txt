6) Energy, commodities and supply dynamics show mixed signals with renewed risk premia.

  - Copper: headlines point to scarcity pricing despite ample supply indicators.  
  - Oil: steady but supported by US‑Iran tensions; China’s Russian oil imports set to hit a new record in February.

7) Technology sovereignty, export controls and sanctions are intensifying global fragmentation.  
  - US findings: Alibaba and BYD linked to Chinese military (formal conclusions announced).  
  - US–Taiwan: new trade agreement to lock in chip investment.  
  - Reports of model copying: Chinese DeepSeek trained by distilling US models; Starlink smuggling and Russia blocking WhatsApp illustrate tech as a theater of state conflict.

8) Political scandals, regulatory action and corporate fallout are triggering leadership changes and legal costs.  
  - $38.3bn: ICE planned spending on detention centers per one document.  
  - $250m: combined pay for six Wall Street bank chiefs in 2025.  
  - Multiple resignations and probes tied to released Epstein files; high‑profile departures at banks and firms.

Current macro/markets regime: We are in an AI-driven structural rotation layered on top of a late‑cycle macro backdrop — central banks are less synchronous, softening US inflation has revived rate‑cut hopes while bond yields and safe‑asset flows remain volatile. Geopolitical and trade tensions are elevating risk premia (energy, defence and tech sovereignty), producing episodic risk‑off moves and rapid sectoral re‑pricing.
