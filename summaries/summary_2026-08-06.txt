  - Gold hit a seven‑week peak / saw its largest daily growth since February as Hormuz talks eased rate‑hike bets; oil prices mixed/slipping on reopening hopes.

7) China and broader geopolitical policy shifts are increasing EM and supply‑chain risk.  
  - China launches a global tax hunt and surprises super‑rich with a tax on offshore trusts.  
  - China imposes countermeasures on US firms and tighter drone export rules; Chinese AI darlings slide on US ban fears.  
  - Samsung and SK Hynix test Chinese chip tools as a hedge against US risks; UK and EU move on Russia/tech sanctions.

Current macro/markets regime: higher-for-longer rate expectations and active policy intervention (FX and sanctions) are colliding with a costly, capital‑intensive AI investment cycle, producing episodic risk-off in growth/tech names and safe‑haven flows into gold and yields; geopolitics (Middle East, China) and supply shocks (energy, weather) are the dominant cross‑currents driving asset dispersion and tactical volatility.
