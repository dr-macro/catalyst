Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a policy‑sensitive, risk‑on market driven by concentrated AI/tech capital flows and major corporate redeployments, but with elevated macro fragility: bond yields and FX are volatile as central banks lean on messaging while political pressure rises. Geopolitical (Middle East, Russia/Ukraine) and climate/supply shocks keep commodity risk premiums elevated, producing a regime of narrow equity leadership, higher tail risks, and frequent rapid repricing in rates, FX and energy.
