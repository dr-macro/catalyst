1) Middle East hostilities are driving a renewed oil supply shock and market volatility.  
  - US renewed strikes on Iran and restarted a naval blockade of Iranian ports.  
  - Oil prices: surged past $80/bbl and touched ~$87/bbl.  
  - Markets pricing higher fuel: Kalshi sees >$4/gal risk; US SPR at multi‑decade low.

2) Europe and Ukraine face rapid remilitarisation and widened defence spending.  
  - Germany to borrow €800bn for rearmament.  
  - Ukraine mounting unprecedented strikes on Russian energy infrastructure.  
  - Black Sea / Ukrainian ports: ~1/3 loss of grain export capacity reported.

3) AI / chip investment cycle is accelerating capex, IPOs and chip-supply frictions.  
  - ASML raised guidance (again) and stock jumped >7%; forecast expansion includes Terafab plans.  
  - Nvidia H200 shipments and restrictions: buyer lists in Asia halved; US official says some H200s shipped to China.  
  - SK Hynix U.S. listing sparked stock moves (shares +13% on one report).  
  - DeepSeek/Thinking Machines/other AI firms planning big listings or funding (DeepSeek ~$74bn valuation cited).

4) Tech sector is bifurcating: AI winners rally while legacy incumbents suffer sharp downdrafts.  
  - IBM shares plunged ~25% after an earnings warning.  
  - AI-related chip and hyperscaler names (ASML, SK Hynix, others) rallied.  
  - Big banks captured strong trading/deal fees as markets heated.

5) Banks and asset managers are benefiting from market activity and flows.  
  - JPMorgan posted its highest quarterly profit ever.  
  - BlackRock assets hit record ~$15.3tn.  
  - Wall Street fees bolstered by large IPOs and mega‑mergers.

6) Macro data and central-bank messaging are mixed, keeping policy risk live.  
  - US CPI cooled to 3.5% year/year in June.  
  - Markets expect a July Fed pause, but Fed leadership (Warsh) emphasizes resolve against hot inflation.  
  - Dollar and Treasury yields reacted (dollar softer after CPI; commentary pointing to resumed curve steepening).

7) Big corporate moves, M&A and IPOs are reshaping market structure and liquidity.  
  - Stripe + Advent offered to buy PayPal for ~ $53bn.  
  - SpaceX IPO experienced a sharp sell-off (stock fell below IPO price; group market cap swing ~ $1tn reported).  
  - Data‑centre firm Switch exploring IPO that could value it up to ~$80bn.

8) China presents a split picture: weak domestic growth but strong export/AI demand and strategic supply tensions.  
  - China Q2 GDP slowed to ~4.3% (weakest since 2022).  
  - Exports and AI‑chip demand surged (ASML upgrades, export wins), while chip controls and licensing (H200 deals, ZTE) complicate flows.  
  - Large onshore IPOs (CXMT / others) targeting multi‑billion dollar raises.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
We are in an AI‑led cyclical reallocation—strong capex and equity flows into chips, hyperscalers and banks—overlaid by a geopolitically driven commodity shock (Iran / Hormuz + Ukraine) that lifts oil and market volatility. Central banks remain data‑dependent: headline inflation has eased but upside energy shocks and sticky core risks keep policy hawkish risk alive, producing higher conditional volatility across rates, FX and commodities.
