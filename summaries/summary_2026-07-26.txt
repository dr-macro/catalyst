Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a commodity‑driven risk‑off regime where geopolitics (Middle East, Houthis, Iran) have pushed oil above $100 intermittently, sparking a global bond sell‑off and repricing short rates higher; at the same time an AI investment boom (huge capex and mega‑deals) is concentrating gains in tech while creating credit/flow stresses (record IG outflows, zombie PE funds) and elevating policy and operational risk — resulting in heightened volatility and a bifurcated market between AI winners and broader macro-sensitive assets.
