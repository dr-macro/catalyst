7) Markets are seeing concentrated private-credit and M&A flows even as investors warn of an earnings bubble and tighten risk controls.

  - Market caution/structure: warnings about an earnings bubble; Goldman banned staff from most prediction‑market trading; Kalshi in talks with regulators over expanded products; activist/hedge moves (Michael Burry buying Flutter/DraftKings).

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a risk‑off, geopolitically driven regime: renewed Middle East hostilities and defence rearmament are lifting commodity prices and breakevens while elevating tail‑risk premia. Central banks and markets are split — higher real-rate starting points and policy uncertainty coexist with heavy AI/tech and defence capex, producing elevated volatility and selective sector leadership rather than broad-based risk appetite.
