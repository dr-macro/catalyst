7. US political moves and policy reversals are creating ongoing regulatory and trade uncertainty that spills into markets.
   - US government decision to fund MAGA‑aligned think‑tanks and charities in Europe.
   - Plans announced to roll back tariffs on metal and aluminium goods.
   - Administration involvement reported in Iran talks and various domestic reversals (e.g., reversal on Moderna flu vaccine block, release of federal project funds).

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a growth‑resilient but policy‑uncertain regime: inflation is cooling unevenly, leaving central banks split between “on‑hold” and conditional cuts while markets price a narrow path to easier policy. Simultaneously, an AI‑driven investment surge and geopolitical headline risk (notably Middle East and Europe defence/tech sovereignty) are producing episodic volatility — tech/AI narratives drive equity rotations while bonds trade with caution around Fed minutes and oil‑risk swings.
