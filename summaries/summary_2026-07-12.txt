   - IEA warns of petrol and diesel supply crunch.
   - Russia’s gasoline output covers 65% of domestic demand after Ukrainian strikes.
   - Utility boss warns US faces blackouts due to power-supply shortfall.

3) AI investment boom is driving hiring, corporate spending, chip demand and concentrated equity moves.
   - Heavy corporate AI spenders add staff faster than peers.
   - Tata Consultancy to hire up to 8,900 AI deployment engineers.
   - Nanya plans $6 billion spending in 2027 for chips.

4) Bond markets and yields are under pressure as higher real rates, larger sovereign borrowing and issuance strains liquidity.
   - Japan’s borrowing costs rose to a 30-year high.
   - Germany to borrow €800 billion for rearmament.
   - Treasury bill issuance has returned as a liquidity test for markets.
   - Multiple rates pieces signal resumed steepening and higher starting real rates.

5) Central bank / inflation uncertainty is rising as policy-makers weigh upside risks from tariffs, conflict and AI-related buildout.
   - Fed officials fret over inflation risk and weigh rate hikes.
   - Fed-report cited “stepped-up” inflation due to tariffs, Iran war, AI buildout.

6) Liquidity, issuance and market-structure frictions are reappearing amid heavy corporate and tech financing.
   - Corporate and sovereign issuance themes covered extensively; corporate bond issuance spotlighted.
   - CME launched a product to simplify controversial Treasury bets.
   - Carlyle selling $2.6 billion data‑centre power unit to EQT for roughly a fivefold return.

7) Europe is accelerating defence spending and strategic autonomy, reshaping fiscal and capital flow patterns.
   - NATO backs Ukraine’s push to hit Russia harder; Finland supportive.
   - Germany to borrow €800 billion for rearmament (historic shift).
   - Germany funding reported 50,000 strike drones for Ukraine (Reuters report).

8) China and EM developments: slower growth, policy tightening in pockets, and capital flows shift around tech and chips.
   - China dropped its urban jobs target amid economic pressures; Q2 slowdown noted.
   - China cracks down on top ratings for corporate bonds.
   - South Korea and Taiwan led a $46 billion emerging-market equity exodus in June.
   - Rare-earths and other strategic-commodity flows highlighted (US mines → Asia).

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher-for-longer interest-rate environment with episodic liquidity strains, driven by heavy AI-related capex and concentrated tech flows on one side and rising geopolitical/energy shocks on the other. Markets are bifurcating: strong demand and valuations for AI/chips versus volatility and repricing risk from higher real yields, large sovereign borrowing and supply disruptions.
