   - Shein shares slid up to ~10% at Hong Kong debut; IPOs/markets mixed
   - Investors rotated into pharma and defensive names as AI winners surge; foreign bonds “taking the lead” over US fixed income in 2026 headlines
   - Record corporate profits reported even as worker pay lags; private‑market redemptions weighing on some fund managers

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a hawkish, risk‑off regime driven by higher real yields and an inflation shock from rising energy prices tied to Middle East hostilities, while concentrated AI-driven gains prop up a narrow equity leadership. Markets are pricing higher-for-longer rates, greater geopolitical risk premia and an uneven growth/inflation mix — favoring quality cyclicals, energy and AI-infrastructure beneficiaries while testing sovereign borrowers and long-duration assets.
