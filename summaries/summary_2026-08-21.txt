Macro/market regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a late-cycle, liquidity‑stressed regime: higher long-term yields and fiscal strain (US debt ~$40tn) have forced large-scale Treasury intervention, leaving bond markets fragile even as pockets of risk appetite (AI, tech, crypto) attract flows. Geopolitical shocks (Middle East, Korea, Ukraine) and energy supply frictions are lifting commodity prices and safe-haven demand, while concentrated AI-related exposures are producing outsized credit and hedge-fund dislocation.
