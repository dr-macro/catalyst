1) Bond-market stress and higher yields are reasserting themselves, tightening liquidity and threatening equity valuations.
 - real yields near 20-year highs
 - Treasury borrowing accelerating; liquidity conditions beginning to tighten
 - rising yields seen as derailing the stock rally
 - risk premium for holding stocks over bonds is vanishing

2) AI and chip investment frenzy is powering concentrated equity gains and huge private financing rounds.
 - Anthropic valuation surges to $965 billion; $65 billion funding deal finalised
 - SoftBank pledges €75 billion for AI facility in France
 - Kirkland & Ellis to spend $500 million on its own AI tech; IBM/Red Hat $5 billion pledge for security initiative
 - Dell shares jump ~30–32% on AI server demand; chip stocks racing to biggest gains since dotcom era

3) Geopolitical shocks — Iran war, Israel/Lebanon escalation and related maritime risks — are driving commodity volatility and second‑round effects.
 - US and Iran move closer to extending ceasefire by 60 days
 - US imposes fresh sanctions on Iran’s military oil sales; US struck a vessel in eastern Pacific (3 killed)
 - Netanyahu orders Israeli forces to take control of 70% of Gaza; Israeli operations expand into Lebanon
 - average US household paying ~$450 more on gas/energy; oil swings (Brent posted biggest monthly loss in six years then later rose >2%)

4) Tech/security export controls, industrial policy and tariffs are fragmenting supply chains and targeting AI compute.
 - US steps to halt Nvidia AI chip shipments to some Chinese firms
 - EU to broaden import quotas and tariffs against China; proposal for crisis powers to seize chip supplies
 - US push to raise North American auto content to 82% under USMCA, with half from the US

5) IPO/speculation wave around AI and space is concentrating investor attention and liquidity into a few headline deals.
 - SpaceX, OpenAI and Anthropic IPOs flagged as potential Wall Street trading frenzy; SpaceX wins $4.16 billion Space Force contract
 - investors racing to get exposure to SpaceX ahead of IPO
 - US stocks posted longest weekly winning streak since 2023; software stocks best month since 2001

6) Energy supply concerns and “shadow fleet” oil flows are elevating inflation risk and real‑rate expectations.
 - Chevron warns oil prices could jump over summer as supplies dwindle
 - EU weighs freezing Russian oil price cap as Middle East war lifts crude prices
 - more than half of shadow-fleet oil tankers pose environmental disaster risk

7) Wage stagnation and distributional pressures are emerging even as corporate profits and AI gains soar.
 - real wages start to shrink in developed countries
 - record divide between corporate profits and worker pay
 - corporate America beginning to ration AI as costs rise

8) Defence spending, military supply stresses and broader strategic competition are intensifying across NATO and the Indo‑Pacific.
 - US warns Japan of severe delays in Tomahawk deliveries due to Iran war
 - Japan spent $73 billion on yen‑buying intervention; Japanese bond yields highest in ~40 years
 - AUKUS to develop unmanned undersea vehicles with delivery set for 2027; China building launch pads near missile silos

Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a risk-on equity regime concentrated in AI/chips, riding liquidity and hype, but it sits atop rising real yields, tightening Treasury issuance/market liquidity and elevated geopolitical/energy tail risk — a fragile environment where a further rise in rates, oil or conflict could trigger broad repricing.
