Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a policy- and tech-driven market regime: aggressive fiscal/administrative actions and massive AI capital flows are pushing risk assets and commodities higher even as real-economy signals show soft hiring and growth. That mix produces stretched valuations, liquidity and sector rotations, with elevated tail‑risk from geopolitics and supply-chain scarcities (memory, copper, shipping) — a market that can reprice quickly to policy or shock news.
