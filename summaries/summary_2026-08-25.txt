  - Bitcoin rally: >$80,000 (over three‑month highs; recent +3% moves reported).

  - EU approved €6.1bn more for Ukraine defence as energy/attack disruptions continue.

  - Market‑fragility indicators spiked; prediction markets skeptical of buyback efficacy.
  - SEC subpoenas into bank links with AI hedge‑fund near‑failure.

10. Defence and regional conflicts remain active tail‑risks that continue to influence allocation to defence, commodities and EM exposure.
  - Ukraine: Patriot launchers reportedly running empty; Europe and allies pledging more defence support.
  - North Korea launched missile barrages; other regional military incidents and strikes reported.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a high‑volatility, dispersion regime: policy and fiscal activism (Treasury buybacks, large sanctions) collide with concentrated AI/tech-driven growth bets, while geopolitical shocks (Iran war, shipping risks) and higher real yields keep risk premia elevated. Safe‑haven flows (gold, crypto) and sector rotation are prominant, with liquidity and credibility of official market fixes now a central market risk.
