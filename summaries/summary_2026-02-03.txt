   - U.S. Treasury yields rise as data reassures growth.

2) AI boom is driving massive corporate financing, M&A and a violent re‑rating of software/data stocks.
   - SpaceX acquires xAI in a $1.25 trillion deal (share exchange ratio 0.1433).  
   - Apple buys Israeli start‑up Q.AI for close to $2 billion.  
   - Amazon in talks to invest up to $50 billion in OpenAI.

6) Labor market and growth signals are mixed: layoffs, rising long‑term unemployment, but resilient activity.

   - Jobs-report timing disrupted (delay due to partial government shutdown); manufacturing shows mixed signals.

7) Dollar, reserve currency dynamics and strategic decoupling trends are accelerating global re‑alignment.
   - Headline analysis: "Termites are slowly feasting away at the foundations of the dollar’s dominance."  
   - Xi calls for the renminbi to attain reserve status; Europe and US push strategic trade / defence deals.  
   - JP Morgan and others signalling rising demand for hard assets; central banks reined in gold purchases even as investment demand soars.

Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a growth-with-tightening regime: growth data remain resilient but markets are highly sensitive to policy and fiscal decisions, producing rising real yields and curve re‑steepening. Simultaneously, an AI‑fuelled capital cycle (large financings, M&A, tech re‑ratings) and episodic geopolitical shocks are driving elevated cross‑asset volatility, especially in commodities, crypto and rate‑sensitive sectors.
