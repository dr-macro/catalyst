Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a late-cycle, policy‑uncertainty environment: inflation is moderating but services/rent pressures persist, markets are priced for eventual Fed easing while political attacks on central‑bank independence and geopolitical shocks (Iran, Venezuela, Greenland tensions) elevate risk premia. That mix is driving safe‑haven flows (gold/silver, Treasuries volatility) alongside selective risk appetite in AI, energy and aerospace sectors.
