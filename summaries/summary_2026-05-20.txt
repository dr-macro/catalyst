   - UAE bypass pipeline ~50% complete, due 2027
   - Three supertankers attempted Hormuz crossing

   - 30‑year Treasury yield topped 5.19% (highest since before the financial crisis)
   - Real yields near 20‑year highs
   - Gilts pushed to record highs; bonds extending sell‑off
   - Mortgage rates at multi‑month highs; refinancing and mortgage-cost lags rising

   - Big Tech AI spending ≈ $725bn (pressure on free cash flow)

   - Nvidia: forecast beat and announced ~$80bn+ return to shareholders; Jensen Huang linked to ~$90bn deal activity

4) Geopolitical realignments and military/defence dynamics are elevating policy and risk premia globally.
   - Xi and Putin meetings; Russia‑China ties emphasised
   - US to cut troops in Europe to lowest level since before Ukraine invasion
   - US delays/considers strikes on Iran; Senate advances measure curbing presidential war powers
   - Saudi floated a Middle Eastern non‑aggression pact with Iran

5) Emerging markets and FX are under stress from higher yields and the energy shock, prompting intervention and support measures.
   - Indian rupee plunged for an eighth straight session; investors dumping Indian assets
   - India to execute a $5bn dollar/rupee swap
   - Japan and other foreign governments retreating from US Treasuries in some flows
   - Indonesia central bank delivered a hefty rate rise to defend the rupiah

6) Energy transition, commodities security and rearmament investments are accelerating as supply risks rise.
   - Hedge funds and investors betting on biofuels as oil shocks push demand
   - NATO pressing European arms makers to boost investment/production
   - EU shortlisting tungsten/rare earths for strategic stockpiles
   - NextEra–Dominion tie-ups and other large energy/utility deals cited in the ~$420bn range

7) Major corporate and capital-market events are reshaping liquidity, hiring and sectoral leadership.
   - SpaceX filed for Nasdaq IPO; one hedge fund could receive a ~$20bn stake on IPO
   - Citi struck a €15bn private-lending partnership with BlackRock; Blackstone unveiled ~$5bn data‑centre plan
   - Multiple large tech and finance layoffs/reshuffles: StanChart to cut >7,000 jobs; Intuit planning ~17% job cuts; McKinsey and banks reorganising pay/headcount

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an inflation‑and‑risk‑premium regime driven by an energy shock and heightened geopolitical risk, which has pushed real yields and borrowing costs materially higher and prompted FX stress in EMs. At the same time an AI‑led capital concentration is powering tech rallies and large corporate transactions, creating a bifurcated market of concentrated tech winners and broadening liquidity/tightening vulnerabilities elsewhere.
