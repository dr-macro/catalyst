Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑longer, policy‑sensitive regime: central banks are re‑tightening as inflation proves stickier in Europe/UK, while bond markets and fiscal risks (notably in the UK) amplify volatility. Political and regulatory shocks from the US midterms are triggering a rapid risk‑rebalancing — heavy repricing in tech/AI and chips, energy volatility, and episodic safe‑haven flows into gold and government bonds.
