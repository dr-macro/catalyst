Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer real‑rate environment driven by sticky inflation and central‑bank hawkishness, producing volatile rates and periodic risk‑off in growth/tech names. Geopolitical shocks and concentrated AI bets amplify periodic dislocations, while defensive rotation and rising household credit metrics increase downside sensitivity in risk assets.
