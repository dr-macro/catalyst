Current macro/market regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer real‑rates backdrop with sticky growth (slowdown in GDP, still‑elevated yields) and heightened narrative‑driven risk (AI disruption + trade and geopolitical shocks). That combination is producing episodic risk‑off swings: equities (especially software) re‑price quickly, while bonds, gold and energy act as short‑term safe havens and flows into AI infrastructure accelerate structural capital reallocation.
