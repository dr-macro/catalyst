Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a hawkish, risk‑off regime driven by rising real yields and sticky inflation expectations that are pressuring duration and growth multiples, while concentrated sector shocks (AI/Nvidia) and elevated geopolitical/supply‑chain risks amplify volatility and cross‑asset repricing. Liquidity is tighter, event risk is front‑loaded (air‑traffic and cloud outages, legal actions, high‑profile governance failures), and investors are rotating toward higher‑quality yield and de‑levered exposures even as frothy AI IPO valuations continue to attract capital.
