1) A US‑Iran ceasefire and preliminary agreement has sparked a broad risk‑on rally and sharp oil re‑pricing.
- US and Iran agree ceasefire / memorandum of understanding.
- Oil falls to three‑month low; slides ~5%.
- Oil sinks below $80 a barrel.
- Average US petrol price falls below $4.
- US Strategic Petroleum Reserve at lowest since 1983.
- STOXX 600 and Dow hit record highs.
- UK, France, Germany and Italy ready to lift Iran sanctions.
- Deal reportedly includes a ~$300 billion fund (more than half already committed).
- Strait of Hormuz transit backlog may take weeks to clear.

2) SpaceX’s IPO and follow‑on deals have dominated market flows, inflating tech valuations and retail/speculative activity.
- IPO initially raised ~$75 billion (world’s biggest IPO).
- Underwriters exercised greenshoe; IPO haul rose to $85.7 billion.
- Company valuation cited around $2 trillion.
- Elon Musk becomes world’s first trillionaire on debut.
- SpaceX to acquire AI coding agent Cursor for ~$60 billion.
- Large insider/anchor buys: Ron Baron ~$1 billion stake; Gina Rinehart ~$1 billion stake.
- Heavy options and ETF flows tied to SpaceX trading.

3) AI commercialization is colliding with national security and regulatory control, creating material business risk.
- US freezes/limits foreign use of Anthropic’s top models (Mythos).
- NSA reported to have used Anthropic’s Mythos for cyber operations.
- Anthropic dispatches staff to DC and is scrambling to restore access.
- OpenAI spending reported at ~$34 billion last year ahead of an IPO.
- Coalition of state attorneys general investigating OpenAI; legal actions vs Anthropic over pricing/access.

4) Central banks are pivoting to higher/less accommodative policy while liquidity signals show strain in fixed income markets.
- ECB raises interest rates (first increase since 2023).
- Bank of Japan raises rates to 1% — highest since 1995 (31‑year high).
- Kevin Warsh takes the Fed chair role; markets expect a hawkish tilt.
- Investing commentary: liquidity conditions tightening as Treasury borrowing accelerates.
- Nvidia plans to raise ~$25 billion in its corporate bond sale.
- Gold hit a six‑month low as speculative investors exited, then drifted higher as ceasefire eased rate‑hike concerns.

5) Tech and AI M&A, plus concentrated flows, are reshaping market winners and creating short‑term dislocations.
- Salesforce to buy Fin for $3.6 billion.
- Fox agrees to buy Roku (~$22 billion reported).
- Databricks and other tech acquirers expanding into cybersecurity/AI (multiple deals reported).
- Asia hedge funds posted triple‑digit gains in the AI‑led rally.
- Private equity and buyouts generating record windfalls tied to AI value creation.

6) China’s growth softening and strategic economic moves are increasing geopolitical and market uncertainty.
- China retail sales fall for the first time since Covid (May).
- Chinese investors rush to open Hong Kong accounts amid Beijing crackdown.
- China cancels high‑level meetings with the EU.
- China tees up a digital payments system to compete with the dollar.
- DeepSeek (Chinese AI) raises ~$7.4 billion.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
Risk appetite has surged on a de‑escalation in the Middle East, driving equity gains and a rapid re‑pricing of oil, but that reflationary impulse sits alongside a tightening policy backdrop (ECB/BOJ moves, Fed leadership change) and strained liquidity in bond markets. Markets are simultaneously being reshaped by an AI‑driven concentration of capital and escalating regulatory/security intervention in AI and tech — creating higher volatility around policy, funding and valuation resets.
