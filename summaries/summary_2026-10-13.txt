Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer real‑rate regime with renewed bond volatility and a rising term premium, overlaid by geopolitically driven commodity risk that is lifting oil risk premia. At the same time, an AI‑led investment and M&A wave is straining capital markets and insurance/legal frameworks, increasing policy and regulatory uncertainty that amplifies market tail‑risks.
