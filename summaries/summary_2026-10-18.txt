Macro/markets regime summary (2–3 lines)
We are in a late‑cycle, higher‑for‑longer environment: sticky inflation and elevated real yields have repriced bonds, raised funding costs and exposed credit/financing stress—especially in AI/data‑centre finance—while geopolitical risk (Middle East, Russia/Ukraine) is pushing oil and volatility higher; central banks and fiscal deficits complicate policy flexibility and raise downside tail risks for risk assets.
