Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in an AI‑led growth rotation concentrated in tech and infrastructure, financed by large private/public capital initiatives, against a backdrop of elevated geopolitical risk (Middle East/Iran) that is re‑tightening energy markets and sending periodic safe‑haven flows. Inflation has cooled but remains above target, keeping the Fed data‑dependent and bond yields volatile — a regime of asymmetric upside risk to tech/capex and downside risk from geopolitics and policy uncertainty.
