Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a high‑volatility, multi‑risk regime: an AI‑driven investment cycle is turbocharging capex, funding and staffing shifts while triggering national‑security frictions, coinciding with an acute geopolitical shock in the Middle East that is raising commodity and risk premia. Markets are oscillating between equity risk‑off and bond safe‑haven bids, with credit and liquidity stresses (private credit, regional lenders) amplifying policy uncertainty for central banks and investors.
