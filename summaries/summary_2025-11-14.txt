Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a risk‑off, “growth-scare” regime marked by rising real yields and policy uncertainty — investors are de‑rating richly priced tech/AI exposures while rotating toward commodities and defensives. Central bank guidance is mixed, making rate-cut timing a live debate and leaving markets sensitive to data, geopolitics and liquidity events.
