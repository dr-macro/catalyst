   - Nvidia to acquire Groq assets for about $20 billion  
   - Nvidia to license Groq technology and hire Groq executives  
   - AI boom added ~$500 billion to US tech billionaires' net worth in 2025  
   - AI start-ups raised/stockpiled roughly $150 billion in funding cushions  
   - Google-parent deal: Intersect acquisition ~$4.75 billion  
   - Several infrastructure stocks have more than tripled this year on the AI trade

2) Equity markets remain strong and concentrated, with AI winners pushing indices toward fresh highs even as some legacy names stumble.  
   - S&P 500 eyeing the 7,000 mark  
   - Stocks on track for a third consecutive year of stellar returns  
   - Oracle on pace for its worst quarter since 2001

3) Precious metals are in a powerful rally as investors seek protection amid geopolitical risk and Fed-cut expectations.  
   - Silver jumped ~9%, trading around $75–77/oz  
   - Comex gold rose ~3.85% for the week and hit record highs  
   - Platinum reached record highs

4) Bond markets are pricing a softer rate path and shifting risk premia, making some non‑US fixed income more attractive.  
   - Market premium for the 10‑year yield near a 2.5‑year low  
   - Forecasts show foreign bonds potentially outperforming US fixed income in 2025  
   - US dollar retreating as Fed rate‑cut odds grow

5) Household affordability remains a headline risk — food, housing and transport costs are politically and economically significant.  
   - Car payments now average > $750/month  
   - Rents falling in several major US cities heading into 2026 (a more renter‑friendly period)  
   - Industry changes as weight‑loss pill approvals prompt food product reformulations

6) Betting, crypto and celebrity-driven flows are creating material platform risk and headline market impact.  
   - DraftKings nearly faced a ~$100 million exposure on one boxing match  
   - A single bet of $5.3 million on Pokémon by a celebrity drew attention to collectible/alternative‑asset liquidity  
   - 29 people detained in an expanding betting probe in one country

7) Geopolitical tension and war‑related activity continue to shape markets and safe‑haven demand.  
   - EU agreed a €90 billion loan package to Ukraine  
   - Canada pledged ~$2.5 billion in economic aid for Ukraine  
   - Russia said it shot down 111 Ukrainian drones in three hours

8) China’s economy shows strains even as policy levers refocus on tech and trade resilience.  
   - China’s industrial profits fell at the fastest pace in over a year  
   - Official revisions lowered 2024 GDP final figures  
   - Beijing launching venture funds into “hard technology” and drafting AI interaction rules

9) Operational risks from weather, recalls and tech testing are creating short‑term market dislocations.  
   - US airlines canceled >1,000 flights during a Northeast winter storm (and ~900 additional cancellations reported as disruptions eased)  
   - Waymo paused robotaxis in San Francisco after flood warnings  
   - Hyundai issuing a recall of >51,000 vehicles in the US

Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a late‑cycle, tech‑led bull where concentrated AI/semiconductor gains and strong aggregate growth coexist with elevated geopolitical risk, uneven consumer affordability and a bond market that’s rapidly repricing toward easier policy. That mix favors growth and tech leadership but raises dispersion, liquidity and policy‑sensitivity risks heading into 2026.
