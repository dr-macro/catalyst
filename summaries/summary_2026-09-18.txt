Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑longer policy regime: central banks have resumed tightening, pushing real yields and long‑end rates materially higher and forcing a broad repricing of duration-sensitive assets. That squeeze is colliding with energy‑driven inflation risks, elevated geopolitical/defence spending and concentrated tech/AI froth — creating a volatile backdrop where liquidity, cybersecurity and regulatory shocks matter as much as growth signals.
