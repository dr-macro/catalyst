  - Fed delivered first hike since 2023 and policymakers forecast another hike

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a tightening, risk‑off inflection: rising real yields and hawkish central banks are colliding with an energy‑driven inflation shock and elevated geopolitical risk, producing bond‑market stress and equity volatility. At the same time, AI is a dominant structural theme—huge capital flows and regulatory/safety uncertainty are driving sectoral re‑pricing and idiosyncratic operational risk.
