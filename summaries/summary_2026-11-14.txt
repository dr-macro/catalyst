Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a tightening, risk-off regime: higher real yields and sticky inflation data are forcing a repricing across bonds and risk assets, while an exogenous governance/shock in the AI sector has amplified equity volatility and disrupted thematic flows. Geopolitical friction and tech-infrastructure fragility add downside tail risks even as big AI funding and IPO activity keep pockets of capital aggressive.
