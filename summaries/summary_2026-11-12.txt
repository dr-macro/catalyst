Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑longer rates regime driven by persistent inflation prints and hawkish central‑bank pricing, with sovereign yields repricing and duration punished. Market volatility is amplified by concentrated AI/tech exposures, governance/security shocks (notably Nvidia), and elevated geopolitical energy risks, prompting a defensive rotation and tighter credit conditions beneath the surface.
