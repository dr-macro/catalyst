Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer rates environment with elevated volatility: sticky inflation (energy‑driven) and geopolitical shocks are keeping yields up while pressuring risky, rate‑sensitive tech/AI positions and data‑centre credit. Liquidity and credit risk are rising at the intersection of rapid AI capex and stretched private financing, prompting sector rotation into defensives and safe havens.
