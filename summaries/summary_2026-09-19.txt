Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a hawkish, tightening regime: central banks have shifted to higher-for-longer rates, driving a broad re‑pricing of fixed income and increasing funding stress for leveraged sectors. Markets are simultaneously exposed to elevated geopolitical/energy risk and concentrated AI-driven tech disruption — producing higher volatility, flows into US equities and large cap growth, and acute cross‑sector idiosyncratic risks.
