Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a higher‑for‑longer rates regime with tightening financial conditions — rising yields are repricing credit and real‑estate risk while creating cross‑asset volatility. Simultaneously, an aggressive private/public AI investment cycle is concentrating capital and operational risk in tech and data‑centre ecosystems, all against an uncertain geopolitical/energy backdrop that can quickly swing markets.
