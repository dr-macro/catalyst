Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a tightening regime driven by a sharp repricing of long yields and higher real rates that are compressing risk premia and stressing leveraged borrowers even as growth remains resilient; simultaneously an AI investment boom is fueling capital demand and infrastructure strain, while elevated geopolitical risks (Middle East, Houthi attacks, US‑China friction) keep commodity volatility and risk premia elevated. Expect greater dispersion: growth‑sensitive assets and debt‑heavy strategies are most exposed, while safe‑yield assets and real assets are being re‑priced.
