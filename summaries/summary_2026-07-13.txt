  - IEA warned of petrol and diesel supply crunch as Russia cuts exports.  
  - Global crude inventories and strategic reserves being drawn to multi‑decade lows.

  - Central bank officials warned that persistent “hot” core inflation could trigger further hikes.  
  - Treasury bill issuance and liquidity tests are returning to market focus.

  - EU imported record LNG volumes from Russia’s flagship plant (reported).

9) Political and policy uncertainty is elevated globally, amplifying market tail‑risk.  
  - US Senator Lindsey Graham died aged 71.  
  - US administration announced renewed maritime enforcement/actions around Hormuz starting imminently.  
  - High‑profile tech and AI legal fights (Apple v. OpenAI) and regulatory moves in Europe add policy risk.

Current macro/markets regime: Elevated geopolitical risk (Middle East and Europe) is creating commodity supply shocks that are keeping inflationary pressures elevated while central banks remain vigilant, leading to higher real yields and episodic risk‑off moves. Equity leadership is bifurcated — AI and semiconductors drive headline growth but face funding and sentiment volatility — so positioning favors liquidity, shorter duration and selective quality exposure.
