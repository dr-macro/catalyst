Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer rate regime with elevated real yields, rising bond volatility and a pronounced risk premium tied to geopolitics and energy; simultaneously, AI capex is fueling a credit/valuation bifurcation across data‑centre and neocloud assets that is stressing credit markets while rotating equity flows into fewer profitable tech names. Risk assets are sensitive to rates, oil and policy surprises — expect episodic repricing and increased dispersion across sectors.
