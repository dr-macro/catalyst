Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a risk‑on, narrow‑leadership equity market driven by outsized AI/Big Tech flows and strong corporate earnings, while higher‑for‑longer rate expectations and dollar strength keep bond markets and real‑economy indicators cautious. Policy and geopolitical shocks (tariffs, OPEC+ moves, U.S.–China frictions, SNAP/government funding) are the main tail‑risk vectors that could quickly reprice risk assets.
