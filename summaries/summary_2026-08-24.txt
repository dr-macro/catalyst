Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑longer yields regime with heightened fiscal credibility risk and active policy intervention in bond markets, while equity markets remain bifurcated — concentrated gains in AI winners and steep volatility elsewhere. Geopolitical (Iran/Hormuz, Ukraine) and cyber risks are elevating risk premia, and markets are re‑pricing capital‑intensive AI infrastructure, trade‑policy shocks and credit stress in real time.
