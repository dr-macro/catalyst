Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an AI‑led, concentration‑driven equity market facing rising tail risk from an intensifying US–Iran conflict that is lifting oil and risk premia; bond markets are showing steepening and liquidity stress as real yields climb. Policy and geopolitical shocks (tariffs, rare‑earths, fiscal shifts) are increasing dispersion and episodic volatility, favouring dynamic risk management and focus on energy, defence, and AI‑infrastructure exposures.
