Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
We are in a risk‑off, policy‑uncertain regime marked by elevated geopolitical tail‑risk, persistent inflation that keeps yields higher for longer, and concentrated technology capital flows driven by AI investment. Market movements are being shaped by liquidity and structural credit strains (private credit), trade/tariff policy shocks, and sectoral divergence between AI winners and stressed cyclicals.
