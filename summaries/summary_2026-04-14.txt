Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a risk‑off, higher‑for‑longer monetary environment overlaid with a large, supply‑side energy shock from Middle East hostilities that is re‑allocating capital into commodity and trading plays while pressuring real growth and trade. Markets are volatile and bifurcated: concentrated tech and banking strength alongside broadening inflationary and geopolitical risks that keep central banks and fiscal authorities on edge.
