Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a geopolitical shock regime: an energy‑driven inflation shock and supply‑chain dislocations coexist with resilient corporate earnings and AI‑led risk appetite, producing episodic volatility. Central banks are cautious (rate cuts pushed out), bond markets are actively repricing policy and risk, and commodity/energy prices are the dominant near‑term macro pivot.
