1) Global bond rout is repricing long-term rates and borrowing costs.
   - US 10‑yr Treasury hit multidecade highs (reported as 19‑yr then 24‑yr highs).
   - US 30‑yr Treasury at highest since 2002.
   - UK 30‑yr gilt >6% (first time since 1998).
   - 30‑yr mortgage ~7.3%; mortgage rates posted largest weekly rise in four years.
   - Corporate borrowing squeezed (high costs cited for large deals).

2) AI boom driving massive financing and deployments while triggering safety and regulatory pushback.
   - OpenAI delays IPO and scraps next model over safety concerns.
   - FTC / state probes and subpoenas opened into OpenAI and Anthropic.
   - Anthropic's IPO prospectus warns of “existential risks.”
   - Broadcom to lend / lease up to $42bn to Anthropic.
   - ElevenLabs valuation doubled to $22bn.
   - Nvidia seeking insurers to spread AI build‑out risk; Micron data‑centre revenue up ~11x.

3) Middle East conflict and Houthi activity are creating oil‑market and trade frictions.
   - Three oil tankers struck in Strait of Hormuz.
   - Middle Eastern crude exports rebounded to prewar / highest levels since Iran war began (but volatility persists).
   - Brent approached ~$100; analysts raised 2026 oil forecasts.
   - EU spent ~€18bn in 2026 to cushion energy price jump.
   - US warns Houthi links to al‑Shabaab threaten Red Sea trade.

4) Tech infrastructure and chip supply are highly concentrated, forcing large commercial commitments and policy responses.
   - Oracle contractually liable to pay data‑centre investors even if sites lack power.
   - Tencent leased 100,000 chips from Oracle.
   - ASML remains sole maker of key lithography machines.
   - Japan plans ~$140bn AI data‑centre push; Broadcom/ Synopsys / AWS multi‑billion AI deals noted.

5) Market leadership remains tech/AI‑centric even as real economy strains show through housing, consumer sentiment and corporate stress.
   - Consumer confidence fell in September amid higher oil and inflation worries.
   - US jobless claims at historic lows but factory‑gate pressures rising.
   - Housing affordability hit as mortgage costs surge; UK homeowners flagged as hit by bond sell‑off.
   - IPO market and dealmaking cooling; some IPOs (Oura) postponed.

6) Geopolitical, fiscal and policy shifts are increasing uncertainty and driving reallocations.
   - US withdraws remaining troops from Iraq.
   - Pentagon awards Boeing ~$20bn Navy stealth fighter contract.
   - Treasury auto‑enrolled ~60 million children in new accounts; Trump announces $200bn South Korea investment pledge.
   - Sanctions and export controls broaden (Iran, Russia); diesel stock release and export‑ban talk active.

Current macro/markets regime — short summary:
We are in a higher‑rate, higher‑volatility regime: long‑term real yields have repriced sharply, tightening financial conditions and lifting financing costs even as growth signals remain mixed and tech/AI‑driven earnings support equity leadership. Overlaying that are elevated geopolitical risks (Middle East, sanctions) and concentrated supply/talent constraints in AI/chips that keep downside tail risks for credit and risk assets elevated.
