Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a risk‑off, bond‑market‑led regime: stronger data and political uncertainty are keeping rates elevated and amplifying volatility, while an AI‑driven sector re‑rating and heightened geopolitical risk push flows into safe havens. Policy and fiscal stress (notably UK gilts) plus accelerating regulatory scrutiny of tech mean markets remain sensitive to headline shocks and rapid de‑risking.
