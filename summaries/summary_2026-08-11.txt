Current macro/markets regime
Risk-on equity and AI-led investment momentum sits alongside a re-pricing of rates and safe assets: yields are volatile as markets wrestle with sticky inflation signals and central-bank messaging shifts, while geopolitical stress (Middle East, shipping) is boosting oil and risk premia. Policy and fiscal moves — plus large tech/AI financing — create a bifurcated backdrop of growth/tech optimism and higher-for-longer rate risk.
