Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an AI-fuelled growth/rotation concentrated in mega‑cap tech amid elevated market volatility, while a stressed bond market (driven by rising long yields and an active Treasury) clashes with fiscal strain (US public debt ≈ $40tn). Geopolitical tail risks (Iran/Strait of Hormuz, trade spats) and supply shocks (energy, weather) raise downside risk for real activity even as pockets of tech-driven investment surge.
