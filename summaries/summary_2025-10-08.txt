Current macro/markets regime — concise view:  
We’re in a high-uncertainty, bifurcated market: a narrow tech/AI-led risk-on tape concentrated in chips and cloud compute, and a parallel safe‑haven bid pushing gold and silver to record levels as political shocks, central-bank policy divergence and fiscal risks (shutdown) elevate tail‑risk premia. Liquidity and policy signaling remain key drivers; positioning should respect dispersion between growth beneficiaries (AI/hyperscale) and macro‑sensitive cyclicals/financials.
