8) Political instability and corporate/legal developments are adding regional policy risk and reallocating capital flows.  
  - Ukraine: defence minister sacked; new army chief; Ukraine recaptured ~700 sq km this year (outgoing general).  
  - Europe: EU agrees 21st sanctions package vs Russia but Greece blocks new measures to protect shipping; ECB/UK political shifts (Burnham) affecting gilt sentiment.  
  - Regulators & fines: Google hit with ~€890m / ~$1bn EU fines; Oracle facing up to $7bn collateral claim for US data centre; antitrust and audit scrutiny rising.

Current macro/markets regime: Elevated geopolitical risk (Middle East + regional spillovers) has triggered a commodity‑led inflation scare that’s lifting real yields and stressing bond liquidity, forcing a risk‑rotation from long-duration growth names into energy, defence and safe‑haven assets. Central banks are caught between sticky inflation risk and uneven growth — market pricing now reflects higher short‑term rate risk, wider credit/CF liquidity concerns, and heightened policy/tariff uncertainty.
