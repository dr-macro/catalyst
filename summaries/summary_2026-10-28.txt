  - Bank of Canada: hiked; BoC said another hike possible
  - RBNZ surprised markets with a hold
  - Reuters poll: high inflation expected to persist into next year
  - Gold > $4,000 (as markets awaited Fed)

6) Market internals show uneven liquidity and concentrated positioning, with credit and private‑debt risks surfacing.
  - Two AI chipmakers accounted for ~34% of US earnings boom
  - Margin debt growth slowed sharply
  - A $300bn “debt wall” cited for private‑equity‑backed companies
  - Insurers and private markets bullish even as government bond yields rise

Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a high‑rate, high‑geopolitical‑risk regime with sticky inflation expectations and elevated real yields, while an AI‑driven tech cycle concentrates equity gains and idiosyncratic downside. Cross‑asset volatility is elevated: bonds offer attractive nominal yields (~5%) but remain sensitive to inflation and policy convictions, commodities and energy prices carry heightened risk premia, and market positioning is uneven and concentrated.
