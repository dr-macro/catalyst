- Volatility: “fear gauge” (VIX) hitting 2026 lows even as bond yields climb.

- US national debt highlighted near ~$40tn.
- Foreign holdings of US Treasuries fell in June (led by Japan, UK, China).
- Investors and headlines: “people worried about America’s solvency”; investors demanding higher returns to lend.

6) China’s growth softness and commodity re‑shaping are altering global trade and corporate winners.

7) Market micro‑risks and credit strains: large trading losses, private‑credit stress and regulatory frictions are surfacing.

- Private credit: troubled loans swelling; pressures noted across the space.
- Banking/regulatory: JPMorgan debanked Polymarket over regulatory worries; banks reached $86.4m Mexican bond rigging settlement.

8) Central bank uncertainty and inflation risk complicate policy outlooks amid geopolitical shocks.
- Reuters poll: economists expect Fed to hold rates this year.
- Market moves: dollar weakening as hike bets dwindle; gold rising on easing Fed fears.
- Bond market commentary: 10‑year yield premium rising on inflation and Fed uncertainty; ECB economists warn of AI‑driven tech correction.

Macro/market regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑longer real‑rates regime driven by a global bond sell‑off and fiscal premium repricing, with oil/Geopolitical shocks adding inflation and growth risk. At the same time, concentrated AI and data‑centre capital is supporting mega‑cap equities even as credit strains, regulatory risk and episodic market losses increase systemic fragility.
