Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑long inflation and rates regime driven by sticky price data and central‑bank tightening, with bond markets pricing materially higher terminal yields; concurrently, geopolitical shocks (Middle East/Red Sea) are creating commodity‑led supply shocks that exacerbate inflation and market volatility, while AI acceleration and its security/regulatory frictions are reshaping capital flows into tech and infrastructure assets. Risk premia have risen across sovereigns, energy and EM, and policy/political uncertainty is amplifying episodic market dislocations.
