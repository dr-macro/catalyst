Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑volatility, higher‑uncertainty regime where rapid AI‑driven investment and concentrated equity gains coexist with active geopolitical shocks that re‑link commodity prices and interest‑rate expectations. Bond markets and select central banks are pushing back on policy complacency, producing abrupt flow shifts (outflows from IG bonds, safe‑haven bids) and frequent risk‑on/risk‑off reversals.
