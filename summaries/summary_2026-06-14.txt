1) Central banks tightening and rates staying sticky, pressuring safe-assets and liquidity.
- ECB raises interest rates for the first time since 2023.  
- Bank of Japan set to hike rates to a 31‑year high.  
- Liquidity conditions beginning to tighten as Treasury borrowing accelerates.  
- Gold sinks to a 6‑month low / heads for a second weekly loss on rate expectations.

2) AI regulation and competition are creating abrupt market and operational shocks.
- Anthropic disables access to top models Fable 5 and Mythos 5 after US restrictions.  
- US blocks foreign access to some Anthropic models; Anthropic staff to meet White House.  
- NSA reported to be using Anthropic’s Mythos for cyber operations.  
- OpenAI plotting a major ChatGPT overhaul and considering drastic price cuts; state AGs investigating OpenAI.  
- Amazon/other corporate concerns helped trigger U.S. restrictions on Anthropic.

3) A historic, market‑moving mega‑IPO is reshaping flows and sentiment toward growth/tech.
- SpaceX IPO priced at $135 and raised ~$75 billion.  
- SpaceX market cap topped ~$2 trillion on debut; shares jumped ~19% on first day.  
- BlackRock placed at least a $5 billion order; heavy retail demand and notable platform demand noted.  
- SpaceX debut lifted market sentiment even as some space stocks later cooled.

4) Middle East tensions moved from escalation toward a tentative deal, sharply easing oil tail‑risk.
- US and Iran reported to have reached a peace deal with signing set for Friday (reported 14 June).  
- Brent crude fell to its lowest since March; oil hit a three‑month low on peace hopes.  
- Iran draft reportedly includes an oil sanctions waiver.  
- Iran said ~20,000 people left without water after US strikes on reservoir tanks (reported).

5) Liquidity and credit strains emerging in private markets amid re‑pricing of risk.
- A BlackRock private‑credit fund honoured less than 40% of redemption requests.  
- That same fund faced ~13% redemption requests in another report.  
- Investors retreating from oil markets at record pace; warnings that defaults are starting to re‑emerge.

6) Geopolitics and industrial policy are driving defence and supply‑security spending decisions.
- Germany unveils a ~€100 billion bid to fix rail infrastructure and reliability.  
- UK forces boarded/intercepted a sanctioned Russian “shadow fleet” oil tanker in the English Channel.  
- US to provide a $4 billion loan to Poland for arms purchases; EU/UK deepen tech and investment ties.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a late‑cycle, higher‑for‑longer rates regime with central banks turning hawkish while liquidity is being strained by rising Treasury issuance; risk appetite is bifurcated — episodic risk‑on driven by megacap/AI and the SpaceX IPO, but credit and private‑market redemptions are forcing re‑pricing of illiquid assets and keeping volatility and event risk elevated. Geopolitical developments (Iran deal + ongoing regional/defence tensions) remain the key near‑term swing factor for oil and risk sentiment.
