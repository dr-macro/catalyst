Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in an AI‑capital‑intensive expansion coupled with higher real yields and episodic risk shocks — FX activism and geopolitical energy supply strains are amplifying volatility. Markets are being driven by heavy tech capex and commodity/inflation sensitivity while central banks and governments oscillate between tightening signals and interventionist backstops, producing rapid sector rotations and policy uncertainty.
