Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
Higher-for-longer real rates combined with an AI capex cycle that is both fueling and fracturing risk assets have produced elevated volatility across equities, credit and commodities. Energy and geopolitics (Middle East, Taiwan/China, supply squeezes) are adding inflationary and tail‑risk premia, so positioning should favor liquidity, convexity protection and selective exposure to secular AI infrastructure winners.
