Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer interest‑rate and elevated real‑yield environment driven by a hawkish Fed narrative, with rising long‑term yields amplifying fiscal costs and volatility. That backdrop is being overlaid by geopolitically driven commodity/energy risk premia and concentrated, AI‑fuelled tech gains — increasing the importance of policy moves and regulatory risk in market pricing.
