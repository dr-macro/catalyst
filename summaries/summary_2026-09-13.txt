Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a risk regime defined by sticky inflation and tighter monetary policy priced into higher long‑term yields, overlaid with a geopolitically driven commodity shock (oil/shipping) that is amplifying inflation and market volatility. Simultaneously, accelerated AI deployment and its safety/governance fallout are creating structural political and credit risks even as tech‑led earnings continue to support equity valuations.
