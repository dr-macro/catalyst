Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a risk‑divergent regime: AI investment and capex are driving concentrated tech and chip outperformance while sticky inflation and central‑bank caution keep real rates elevated and bond volatility high. At the same time, elevated geopolitical risk (US‑Iran, Ukraine) is boosting commodities and safe havens, and liquidity stress in private credit is producing episodic market dislocations.
