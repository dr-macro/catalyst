Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
Liquidity and policy expectations are shifting toward easier central‑bank bias (QT tweaks, rate‑cut speculation) while markets are led by concentrated AI/Big‑Tech gains and heavy corporate financing tied to the AI buildout. That combination has produced a narrow, profit‑driven rally with elevated issuance and geopolitical/policy risks that could re‑test breadth if growth or policy signals change.
