Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher-for-longer rates regime driven by a global repricing of duration and fiscal stress, with bond yields and sovereign borrowing costs forcing a reassessment of risk across credit and equities. Simultaneously, commodity and geopolitical shocks (energy, Middle East, sanctions) and a techno‑industrial boom in AI/chips are creating cross‑asset dispersion—rewarding capex/AI winners while testing credit, duration and policy bandwidth.
