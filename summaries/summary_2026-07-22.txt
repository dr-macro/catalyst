Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
We are in a risk‑off, bifurcated regime: geopolitics (Iran/Houthi/Ukraine) and energy shocks are lifting commodity and safe‑haven premia while AI‑driven tech capex and M&A concentrate equity and liquidity flows into a narrow set of winners. Central banks remain constrained by sticky inflation and higher real rates, leaving bond markets volatile and FX intervention risk (eg. yen) elevated.
