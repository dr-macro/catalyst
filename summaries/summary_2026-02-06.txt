Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a risk‑off, cross‑asset repricing driven by a tech/AI capital‑spend shock, rising geopolitical tail‑risks and renewed bond‑market volatility; central banks are unevenly positioned, leaving a steepening‑and‑volatile yield curve backdrop. Equity weakness is concentrated in growth/AI exposed names while safe havens (Treasuries, dollar) and real‑asset volatility (commodities, margins) have picked up — liquidity and policy uncertainty are the dominant short‑term market risks.
