Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a higher‑for‑longer monetary regime being priced by markets after a hawkish Fed signal, coinciding with elevated geopolitical risk (Middle East energy disruption) and concentrated tech‑driven earnings growth from AI. That mix is producing higher yields and dollar strength, increased policy intervention (FX, sanctions) and greater dispersion across sectors — cyclicals and energy exposed to geopolitics, AI winners capturing equity upside while broader growth indicators show consumer strain.
