   - Nepal/China rescue operations and lake burst risks prompted evacuations and halted rescue work  
   - Russia‑Ukraine air war intensifying with lethal depot strikes; NATO/Fed/Treasury actions drawing political pushback

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a hawkish, supply‑constrained regime: central banks (and influential officials) are leaning toward tighter policy, which is lifting real yields and pressuring rate‑sensitive assets even as AI and chip winners concentrate equity returns. Elevated geopolitical and commodity risks are amplifying volatility and pushing investors toward selective safe‑haven and foreign‑fixed‑income plays while credit/corporate‑balance dynamics complicate market liquidity.
