9) Central‑bank and policy expectations are mixed: "higher for longer" priced in, with divergence across regions.  
   - Markets: consensus that Fed will skip October but raise in December (most economists)  
   - China: loan rates left steady for 17th consecutive month  
   - BoJ reported to consider raising inflation forecast while standing pat on rates; global rate rises amplifying cross‑market effects

Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a high‑rate, high‑uncertainty regime: real yields and long‑end rates are substantially re‑priced higher, amplifying volatility across bonds, credit and rate‑sensitive assets. An AI‑driven capex boom is concentrating funding and credit risk in data‑centre/AI pockets even as Middle East shocks raise energy prices and geopolitical risk, producing uneven sector‑level rotations and a fragile risk‑on/risk‑off backdrop.
