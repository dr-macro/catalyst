Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
Markets are in a bond‑market‑led tightening phase: stronger U.S. jobs and geopolitical risk have pushed yields sharply higher, repricing risk premia and tightening financial conditions even as AI‑driven tech investment fuels concentrated corporate profits and heavy capex (data centres). That mix raises recession/credit risk down the cycle while keeping inflation and policy uncertainty elevated.
