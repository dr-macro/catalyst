Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
The market sits in a late-cycle, policy‑sensitive regime: slowing US job growth has softened immediate Fed tightening odds and lifted bond and risk sentiment, even as central banks signal vigilance. Simultaneously, AI-driven investment and M&A are concentrating market leadership while geopolitical and energy shocks sustain dispersion and idiosyncratic risk across sectors.
