  - Anthropic valuation: $965 billion  
  - Anthropic funding deal: $65 billion  
  - Apollo/Blackstone working on ~$36 billion debt package for Anthropic  
  - Chip stocks racing to biggest gains since dotcom era; Nvidia central to rally  
  - Dell: sales +88% and shares rose ~30% on AI data‑centre demand  
  - Samsung shipped HBM4E chip samples; energy constraints pushing AI chip design changes

2) Rapid product rollouts and weak guardrails are forcing regulatory and ethical debates about AI.  
  - Meta/Google guardrails stripped in minutes (models)  
  - Pope compared AI risk to the Tower of Babel  
  - EU pushing “tech sovereignty” and broader import/quotas/tariffs vs China  
  - Kirkland & Ellis to spend $500 million on AI tech; IBM/Red Hat pledged $5 billion for AI security

3) Bond markets and real yields have reasserted control, tightening financing conditions and raising rate risk.  
  - Real yields near 20‑year highs  
  - Treasury borrowing accelerating and liquidity beginning to tighten  
  - Investors pouring into short‑dated bond funds (inflows reported)  
  - Analysis: markets still exposed to more upside in rates; “risk premium” for stocks over bonds is narrowing

4) Middle East conflict and related sanctions have injected energy‑security shocks and elevated commodity volatility.  
  - US/Iran framework for 60‑day ceasefire under discussion; requires US approval  
  - US imposed fresh sanctions on Iran’s military oil sales  
  - Netanyahu ordered control of 70% of Gaza; Israeli operations expanded  
  - Exxon warns inventories could hit “dangerously low” levels; Chevron warns oil prices may jump over summer  
  - Brent/oil: biggest monthly drop in May (~20%) on hopes of a ceasefire, but supply risk remains

5) Space, defense and strategic sectors are unusually active—IPO mania, big contracts and spectacular failures.  
  - SpaceX IPO frenzy and valuation talk (Musk trillionaire narratives)  
  - SpaceX won $4.16 billion Space Force contract  
  - Blue Origin’s New Glenn rocket exploded on the launchpad  
  - Danish pension fund excluded SpaceX over governance concerns

6) Policy‑driven de‑risking and industrial policy are reshaping trade and supply chains.  
  - EU to broaden import quotas and tariffs vs China; pushing tech sovereignty  
  - US push to raise North American auto content (proposal: 82% NA; higher US share)  
  - Eurozone issuers turning to non‑euro debt seeking new investor pools

7) Structural headwinds—demographics and real‑wage pressure—are showing in advanced economies.  
  - Global birth rates falling broadly  
  - Real wages beginning to shrink in developed countries  
  - France GDP: −0.1% in Q1; US Q1 GDP revised lower  
  - UK showing signs of an expanding “lost generation” and weak junior hiring

8) Market structure and liquidity dynamics are creating large winners (trading desks, AI‑hardware suppliers) and stress in credit.  
  - Citadel Securities trading revenues: $4.3 billion (record) during Iran volatility  
  - Unrealized losses at US private‑credit lenders have deepened  
  - Dell and other server/chip suppliers saw outsized share moves on AI orders; Anthropic funding driving private markets activity

9) Corporate strategy is increasingly tied to AI and defence demand, fueling M&A, bonuses and reallocation of capital.  
  - Samsung workers to receive $400,000 bonuses after AI profit‑sharing deal  
  - JPMorgan said it could spend up to $20 billion on an acquisition  
  - Kirkland & Ellis: $500 million AI tech buildout; Lilly agreed to buy three vaccine developers (deal announced)  
  - Robinhood enabling AI chatbots for trading and payments

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an AI‑led growth/re‑allocation regime: tech and chip stocks are driving concentrated risk‑on flows even as rising real yields, tighter Treasury liquidity and geopolitics (Middle East supply risks, sanctions) reintroduce inflation and volatility upside. Expect narrow leadership, higher dispersion across sectors and a market that is sensitive to bond yields, energy shocks and policy/regulatory developments.
