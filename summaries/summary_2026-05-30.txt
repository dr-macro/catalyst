1) AI and chip-driven boom is fuelling a concentrated market rally and record private valuations.
  - Anthropic valuation: $965 billion; $65 billion funding deal finalised.
  - Anthropic related financing: Apollo/Blackstone working on $36 billion debt deal.
  - Chip / AI capex: Dell shares +30–33% on AI server demand and raised outlook; chip stocks seeing biggest gains since dotcom era; Nvidia-led ecosystem announcements (board seat, Windows PCs with Nvidia chips).
  - Large investments: SoftBank up to €75 billion for French AI data centres; IBM/Red Hat $5 billion security pledge; Amazon–Snowflake $6 billion deal reported.

2) Bond yields and real yields are rising sharply, squeezing liquidity as government borrowing accelerates.
  - Real yields near 20‑year highs.
  - Treasury supply / liquidity: liquidity conditions tightening as Treasury borrowing accelerates; guidance on how to read auctions highlighted.
  - Market flows: investors pour into short-dated bond funds; warnings that surging bond yields could derail the stock rally.

3) Energy market is volatile from Middle East shocks and supply worries, producing large price swings and supply alerts.
  - Oil price moves: Brent and WTI volatility with oil tumbling ~20% in May (largest monthly drop since 2020) on ceasefire hopes but ongoing supply risks.
  - Producer warnings: Exxon warns inventories will hit “dangerously low levels” in weeks; Chevron warns oil prices likely to jump over summer.
  - Geopolitical impacts: US imposes fresh sanctions on Iran’s military oil sales; Iran/US moving toward 60‑day ceasefire framework (subject to US approval); Saudi may adjust July pricing to Asia.

4) Geopolitical and military escalation remains a central market risk with active theatres and military supply disruptions.
  - Israel / Gaza: Netanyahu ordered Israeli forces to take control of 70% of Gaza.
  - US‑Iran: US ready to restart strikes if no deal; US warns Japan of severe Tomahawk delivery delays due to Iran war.
  - Regional incidents: Russian/Ukraine strikes and drone incidents (including one hitting Romania); US sanctions and military actions reported.

5) Equity markets show narrow, momentum-driven strength despite weaker real incomes and uneven macro data.
  - Market breadth / records: US stocks longest weekly winning streak since 2023; Wall Street reached new closing highs; momentum strategies posted best 2‑month gains on record.
  - Valuation / sentiment strains: risk premium for holding stocks over bonds shrinking; “record disconnect” between market levels and consumer sentiment noted.
  - Corporate earnings / flows: Dell, ServiceNow and several AI beneficiaries led gains; flows into AI‑related trades fuel market leadership.

6) Policy, regulation and trade are tightening around tech, chips and China — raising fragmentation risks for supply chains.
  - EU / trade actions: EU to broaden import quotas and tariffs against China; push for tech sovereignty and crisis powers to seize chip supplies.
  - Guardrails & governance: AI guardrails stripped from Meta/Google models in minutes; increased scrutiny of data / national security (rare-earth tech theft disputes).
  - Industrial policy: US moves to raise North American auto content targets (82% target reported); Hong Kong overtakes Switzerland as offshore wealth hub.

7) Real wages, demographics and labour tensions are resurfacing as structural headwinds to growth and consumption.
  - Income trends: Real wages starting to shrink in developed countries; widening gap between corporate profits and worker pay.
  - Demography: Birth rates falling broadly; coverage warning demography will worsen long‑term trends.
  - Labour outcomes: “Lost generation” and weak junior hiring debated; Samsung workers set for up to $400,000 bonuses under AI‑profit sharing deal (outlier).

8) Financial plumbing and private-credit stress are emerging alongside large corporate/transaction activity.
  - Private credit: Unrealised losses at US private credit lenders deepen.
  - Treasury / fiscal: Accelerating US Treasury borrowing tightening liquidity; several M&A and large deals in play (e.g., CVC $4.3bn buy, Tilman Fertitta $5.7bn Caesars purchase headlines).
  - Market structure: Perpetual futures and regulated crypto derivatives expansion; prediction/perpetual markets growth noted.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a narrow, AI‑driven risk‑on regime: equity leadership is concentrated in AI/semiconductor beneficiaries while real yields and short‑term rates have moved materially higher, tightening liquidity amid accelerating Treasury supply. Geopolitical shocks (Middle East, Russia) and energy‑supply frictions keep inflation and tail‑risk risk elevated, forcing central banks and policymakers into difficult trade‑offs between containing inflation and supporting market stability.
