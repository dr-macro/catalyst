Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a hawkish, cross‑asset risk‑off regime driven by sticky inflation and central‑bank resolve, coincident with elevated geopolitical risk that is keeping oil and safe‑haven flows elevated and pressuring fixed‑income markets. Positioning is being reshaped by an AI investment boom that concentrates corporate profits and capital, while sovereign/fiscal stress and episodic supply shocks raise the probability of tighter financial conditions and higher market volatility into September.
