Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
Risk appetite has shifted constructive as a US–China trade thaw and cooler US inflation pushed markets to price earlier Fed easing, supporting equities and pro‑risk beta while compressing safe‑haven bids. At the same time, a concentrated, capex‑heavy AI cycle (data centres, power, chips) is bifurcating returns and raising valuation and commodity‑supply vulnerabilities, leaving the market sensitive to political/trade headlines and macro liquidity signals.
