Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a bifurcated, AI‑led risk regime: heavy corporate investment into AI is supporting a concentrated risk‑on in winners while triggering rapid de‑rating and credit stress in vulnerable software and credit exposures. At the same time geopolitical shocks (energy and maritime risk) and divergent central‑bank paths are driving episodic safe‑haven flows and elevated volatility across bonds, FX and commodities.
