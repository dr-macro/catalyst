Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a regime of rate‑repricing and heightened risk premia: long‑dated yields have repriced sharply higher even as growth signals are mixed (weak payrolls but persistent inflation pressure), producing bond‑led volatility and funding stress. Simultaneously, AI and tech concentration are driving capital flows and policy scrutiny, while Middle East energy and multi‑front geopolitical risks keep a premium on commodity prices and safe‑haven assets.
