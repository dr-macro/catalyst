Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a high‑volatility, policy‑sensitive environment where elevated real yields and a hawkish‑leaning Fed tussle with episodic risk premia driven by geopolitics and commodity shocks. Markets are bifurcated: concentrated tech/AI winners see explosive flows and sharp reversals, while bond and FX markets (and interventions) are reasserting control over financial conditions.
