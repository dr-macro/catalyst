   - Japan reportedly “months” from running out of critical rare earths as China tightens supply  
   - TSMC searching for factory‑equipment alternatives to blunt China’s rare‑earth leverage  
   - Samsung Electro‑Mechanics receiving ~$2.8bn chip investment (reported)

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a higher‑for‑longer rates environment with elevated volatility driven by competing shocks — sticky inflation/energy risk, a capital‑intensive AI build‑out straining credit markets, and renewed geopolitical uncertainty (Europe/Middle East/Asia). That mix is producing periodic risk‑off flows into sovereigns even as tech and specialist infra assets face funding and valuation stress.
