Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
Risk‑on markets are being driven by strong earnings, tech/AI momentum and growing rate‑cut expectations, while real‑economy and policy forces (China slowdown, supply‑chain onshoring, geopolitics) lift commodity and defence sectors. The backdrop is one of stretched market leadership concentrated in big tech, rising structural bets on critical minerals/AI, and elevated tail‑risk from operational, credit and political shocks.
