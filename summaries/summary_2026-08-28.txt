Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an AI‑led equity concentration regime — mega‑cap tech and chip investments fueling growth — while long‑term rates are being repriced amid record US sovereign debt, active Treasury interventions and an emergent Treasury–central‑bank split. Geopolitical (Iran, Hormuz, DPRK) and climate shocks keep commodity risk premia elevated, producing a bifurcated market: concentrated risk‑on in AI/semiconductors versus wider macro and bond fragility.
