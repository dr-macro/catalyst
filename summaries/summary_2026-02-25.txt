  - US expands sanctions on Iran; various countries reposition diplomatically/militarily.

5) Rates and bond markets are recalibrating: yields near perceived “fair value,” investors taking more maturity risk amid central bank and leadership shifts.  
  - US 10‑year Treasury yields trading near “fair value”; Treasury yield paused at 4.073% around PCE and geopolitical tests.  
  - Multiple pieces on investors embracing maturity risk in 2026 and curve pressures rebuilding at long dates.  
  - Christine Lagarde to leave ECB before end of her term (central bank leadership change).

6) Stress in private credit and alternative assets is spilling into public markets and investor sentiment.  
  - Blue Owl permanently halts redemptions at a private‑credit fund aimed at retail investors.  
  - Private credit stocks slid after the halt; commentary labels structural problems and record private‑equity sale logjams.  
  - Blue Owl fallout triggered retail‑investor panic per reporting.

7) Resource security, export controls and industrial policy are accelerating supply‑chain nationalism and critical‑minerals strategies.  
  - Governments rushing to hoard critical minerals; Zimbabwe bans exports of raw minerals and lithium concentrates.  
  - Japan mining Pacific Ocean mud for rare earths.  
  - China imposes export controls on 20 Japanese entities; DeepSeek/Anthropic/Chinese AI “distillation” disputes reported.

8) Corporate finance activity is heavy and mixed: big buybacks, elevated valuations, sectoral M&A and repricings amid AI and tariff headwinds.  
  - Stripe valued at $159bn in latest share sale.  
  - Salesforce commits $50bn to buybacks while cutting guidance elsewhere.  
  - Paramount raises bid to $31/share for Warner Bros. in takeover skirmish vs Netflix deal; multiple large M&A/valuation moves ongoing.

Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a late‑cycle, policy‑sensitive regime where technology (AI) is the dominant structural theme driving capex, M&A and labor shifts, while trade policy and geopolitical risk intermittently reprice risk‑assets and safe havens. Rates are elevated but range‑bound, leaving bonds relatively more attractive amid equity volatility and concentrated tech-led leadership.
