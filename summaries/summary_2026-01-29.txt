Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a higher-for-longer rates equilibrium with the Fed paused but cautious — real rates still elevated, prompting yield sensitivity across assets. Markets are bifurcated: AI and chip capex are powering equity leadership while classic software and interest-rate‑sensitive names correct; simultaneous commodity strength and a softer dollar reflect risk and geopolitical premia.
