Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an AI‑led equity market rally concentrated in mega‑cap tech and chips, running alongside a renewed bond repricing (higher real yields and front‑end volatility) and stretched liquidity in private credit. Geopolitical flashpoints and accelerating energy/data‑centre capacity constraints are raising supply‑side and policy risks, making macro outcomes more binary and market moves more event‑driven.
