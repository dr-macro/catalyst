Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a “higher‑for‑longer” interest‑rate environment with aggressive repricing of bond and credit risk, while an AI/chips capex boom is concentrating capital and driving large corporate debt issuance — all against a backdrop of geopolitical energy shocks and rising regulatory/legal risk that amplify volatility and sectoral dispersion. Risk assets are bifurcated: concentrated gains in AI/chip winners amid broad market sensitivity to yields, energy shocks and policy moves.
