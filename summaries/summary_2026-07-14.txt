5) Market structure is bifurcating: big‑bank trading/IB fees and defence/energy winners versus tech/chip volatility and stress in private credit.  
- Bank results: Goldman, JPMorgan, BofA, Wells Fargo report trading/IB strength; JPMorgan posts record profit; Goldman beats estimates.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a volatile, commodity‑sensitive regime driven by geopolitical escalation that is lifting energy prices and risk premia while keeping bond yields elevated; at the same time an AI/chip capex boom concentrates market gains and fuels earnings/trading upside for banks. Policy is mixed — cooler headline CPI gives scope to pause on hikes, but central bankers and markets remain sensitive to upside inflation and geopolitical risk, so positioning should prioritize liquidity and convexity.
