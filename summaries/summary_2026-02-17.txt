Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a late‑cycle, high‑uncertainty transition: AI investment and structural demand for chips/compute are colliding with a tech re‑rating and stretched positioning, while central banks inch toward easing conditioned on disinflation. Elevated geopolitical risks and commodity/energy shocks keep safe‑haven flows and episodic volatility, so expect sharper sector rotations and dispersion rather than a broad, steady rally.
