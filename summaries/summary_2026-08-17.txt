Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a bifurcated, AI‑led risk‑on market where massive tech capex and concentrated investor flows lift equities even as the bond market reprices higher long‑term rates; overlaying this is elevated geopolitical risk (Iran/Strait of Hormuz) that intermittently shocks energy and shipping and keeps volatility and dispersion high. Credit and operational stresses (large trading losses, private‑credit strain, insurer/lender exposures to data‑centre projects) raise the odds of episodic market dislocations despite broad risk appetite.
