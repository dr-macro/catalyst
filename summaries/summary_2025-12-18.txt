Current macro/markets regime — succinct view:
We’re in a “selective risk‑on / broad risk‑off” phase: growth assets are being re‑rated (AI winners and cyclicals tied to energy/data‑centre demand), while broader tech faces de‑risking and regulatory scrutiny. Inflation is easing but labour and geopolitical shocks keep policy paths divergent, so investors are splitting allocations between higher‑quality fixed income/precious metals and concentrated equity plays rather than a broad market rally.
