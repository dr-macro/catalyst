Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a late‑cycle environment with still‑robust growth and sticky inflation, so central banks are paused but biased to tighten again if needed — leaving yields volatile and fixed‑income markets susceptible to dislocations. Markets are being reshaped by an AI re‑rating that amplifies equity volatility and corporate capex uncertainty, while geopolitics (energy/shipping) and episodic cyber/crypto shocks add short‑term risk premia.
