Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a higher‑for‑longer rates repricing driven by resilient US labour and stronger headline growth, which is tightening financial conditions and forcing a revaluation of duration and credit. Concurrently, an AI‑led tech boom concentrates profits and funding (and political/community friction around data centres), while geopolitical shocks—especially Middle East/Iran tensions—are amplifying commodity and risk premia.
