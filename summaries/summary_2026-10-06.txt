Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in an AI‑led risk‑on equity environment dominated by a narrow set of megacap winners, while a parallel bond rout (rising long yields) and energy‑supply shocks are creating a highly bifurcated market with stretched valuations, higher funding costs and elevated geopolitical and cyber tail risks — forcing policymakers and investors to juggle growth hopes against inflation, fiscal strain and security risks.
