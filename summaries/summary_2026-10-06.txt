- euro at 16–17‑month lows
- warnings that gilts and French bonds face contagion
- Japan’s bond market flagged as a potential global catalyst

- China closed “hundreds” of banks to shore up the system

6) Investors are bifurcating: equities (especially AI leaders) hit records even as fixed income turbulence creates profit opportunities and portfolio rebalancing.

- Paramount/Warner‑related deals cited in the $81bn–$110bn range
- Schneider Electric acquisition of PTC ~ $23.7bn
- BT bought TalkTalk out of administration; various $5bn+ and €34.5bn deals noted

Current macro/markets regime
We’re in a higher‑yield, higher‑volatility regime where disinflation is contested: long‑dated rates have repriced materially, prompting sovereign and FX stress in Europe while equity markets (led by AI winners) remain elevated — creating a risky narrow market leadership and active repricing of risk premia across credit, energy and geopolitically sensitive assets.
