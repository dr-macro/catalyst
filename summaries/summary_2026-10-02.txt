Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an inflation- and geopolitics-driven regime where higher long-term real yields and funding costs are tightening financial conditions even as AI-led concentration drives equity flows into a handful of megacaps and infrastructure. Expect greater dispersion across sectors, higher volatility in rates/FX/commodities, and recurring policy and regulatory shocks around AI, energy and fiscal stress.
