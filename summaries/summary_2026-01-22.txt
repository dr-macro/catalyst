Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)  
Policy and geopolitics are the dominant cross‑currents: sticky-ish underlying inflation and a Fed leaning toward patience sustain higher-for-longer rate risk, while erratic geopolitical moves (tariff threats then retreats) amplify short-term volatility. Equities remain supported by AI/tech narratives, but safe‑haven assets (gold, gas) and episodic flow shifts mean markets trade on headlines as much as fundamentals.
