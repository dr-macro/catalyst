1) AI/Big‑tech frenzy driving record fund‑raising, hiring and imminent blockbuster IPOs.  
  - Anthropic finalised a $65bn funding deal and confidentially filed for an IPO.  
  - Alphabet to raise $80bn in equity for AI spending.  
  - SoftBank pledges €75bn (WSJ/Reuters also reported ~$52bn) for AI data centres in France; France attracted $108bn foreign investment partly for these projects.  
  - Nvidia unveils an AI “superchip” for PCs; CEO to engage with Chinese academia and partners (Unitree); HPE, Intel, Kirkland & Ellis and others scale AI investments.  
  - SpaceX/OpenAI/Anthropic IPOs expected to ignite Wall Street; SpaceX to reserve ~5% of IPO shares for staff/friends and waive lock‑up.

2) Bond market repricing and liquidity stress are reasserting constraints on risk assets.  
  - Real yields near 20‑year highs.  
  - Japanese bond yields are the highest in 40 years.  
  - Treasury yields rising; liquidity beginning to tighten as Treasury borrowing accelerates.  
  - Multiple pieces warning that surging bond yields could derail the stock rally.

3) Middle East conflict (Iran/Israel/Lebanon) is triggering an energy shock and commodity price volatility.  
  - Oil jumped as much as ~7% to top $97/bbl and moved +2% on other headlines.  
  - Iran threatened closure of the Strait of Hormuz and suspended talks; US and Iran exchanged strikes.  
  - US crude exports hit a record high in May.  
  - Fertiliser producers cut output as Iran war squeezes sulphur supplies.

4) Geopolitical fragmentation and defence buildup are rising across regions, affecting supply chains and spending.  
  - Putin and Xi to discuss a major gas project; Russia reportedly overspent ~$28bn on the Ukraine war.  
  - Nine countries pulled out of a Ukraine ammunition coalition; AUKUS to deliver unmanned undersea vehicles by 2027.  
  - Europe tightening trade/industrial policy (broader import quotas/tariffs vs China; EU cloud/tender rules).

5) Central banks and politics: communication and credibility are in focus as inflation and yields persist.  
  - Fed Chair Powell warned politicising the Fed would erode trust and said the Fed is under a “stress test.”  
  - BOJ may pause its bond taper next year.  
  - ECB/Euro‑area surveys show elevated inflation expectations even as consumers remain relatively benign.

6) Market internals: a narrow, AI‑led equity rally coexists with strong trading profits and concentrated deal activity.  
  - US stocks hit their longest weekly winning streak since 2023; Wall St hovering near record highs led by Nvidia/AI names.  
  - Citadel Securities posted a record $4.3bn in trading revenues on Iran volatility.  
  - Berkshire agreed to buy Taylor Morrison (~$6.8bn reported); multiple large takeover/IPO deals active (MGM, Liftoff, Honeywell/Quantinuum, Blackstone‑backed deals).

7) Real‑economy strains: supply costs and wages show divergence amid tech‑driven export strength.  
  - Real wages starting to shrink in developed countries.  
  - Aluminium at four‑year highs; US beef prices at record highs due to cattle shortages.  
  - South Korea export growth at a four‑decade high on AI chip demand while China factory activity shows mixed signals.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an AI‑led risk‑on market with intense capital flows into tech and big IPOs, but that optimism sits atop rising real yields, tighter Treasury liquidity and a renewed energy/supply shock from Middle East conflict — producing higher volatility and asymmetric tail risks. Policy credibility and geopolitics, not fundamentals alone, are the likely near‑term drivers of asset class performance.
