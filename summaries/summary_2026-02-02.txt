Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a high‑volatility, policy‑sensitive regime: central‑bank uncertainty (Warsh nomination) and heavy AI‑driven capital flows are driving large re‑allocations across rates, FX and equities, while a commodity/metals liquidation has amplified cross‑asset spillovers and hit risk sentiment. Geopolitics and fiscal action in EMs are the next layer of directional risk for oil, FX and cyclicals.
