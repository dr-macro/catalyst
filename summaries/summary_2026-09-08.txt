Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a regime of rising real rates and risk repricing driven by persistent inflation/commodity shocks and geopolitics, with concentrated structural investment into AI and data infrastructure creating sectoral divergence. Fiscal and reserve pressures (sovereign issuance, reserve intervention, large fund repositioning) elevate tail‑risk for bond markets and liquidity, so market volatility and episodic spillovers across FX, credit and commodities are likely to persist.
