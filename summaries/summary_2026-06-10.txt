Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a higher-risk, cross-asset regime driven by an AI-driven capex boom and resurgent geopolitical/energy shocks that are keeping inflation and real yields elevated; liquidity is tightening as sovereign issuance rises and markets oscillate between tech-led euphoria and risk-off on conflict/inflation headlines. Positioning should reflect greater event risk, structural tech exposure, and sensitivity to rates and oil price moves.
