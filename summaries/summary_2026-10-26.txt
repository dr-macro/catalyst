Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
Rates are structurally higher and sticky, forcing a re‑price of duration and growth assets while keeping risk premia elevated; central banks are largely paused but remain data‑sensitive. Concurrently, an overbuilt, costly AI/infrastructure cycle is trimming tech risk appetite, geopolitical energy shocks are intermittently spiking volatility, and EMs are vulnerable to political surprises.
