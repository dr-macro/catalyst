Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a risk‑off repricing phase: rapidly rising real rates and a stronger dollar amid an energy‑led supply shock and elevated geopolitical risk, while the AI sector faces regulatory and demand uncertainty that clouds tech capex. The combination raises recession/inflation trade‑offs and favors defensive positioning, higher short‑term yields and volatility across equities, credit and commodities.
