Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a policy‑sensitive late‑cycle regime: bond markets are re‑testing inflation expectations and government funding dynamics even as a Democratic midterm sweep has softened geopolitical risk premia and sparked a risk‑on rotation into large tech names. Credit and sector dispersion are rising — AI/data‑centre exposure, cyber/crypto losses and China‑trade frictions are key idiosyncratic risk channels to monitor amid still‑sticky inflation and central‑bank hawkishness.
