Current macro/markets regime — 2–3 lines:
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer rates regime driven by resilient growth and a massive AI investment cycle that is sucking up capital and credit, while energy tightness and rising geopolitical risk elevate risk premia. That combination is producing a bond‑led volatility shock, deepening cross‑asset dispersion (big winners in AI equities vs. broad market and credit stress) and rising scrutiny of tech balance‑sheet leverage and AI governance.
