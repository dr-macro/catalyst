1) Rates and bond-market stress: markets are recalibrating to a higher-for-longer policy path with renewed volatility around the 5% area.
   - US 10-year trading around 5% (multiple bond headlines).
   - Headlines warning “don’t expect a break back below 5%” and questioning whether bonds are pricing a Fed policy mistake.
   - Treasury yields logged the biggest drop since the 2023 banking crisis (MarketWatch).
   - Norway wealth fund posted a $30bn loss after bond-market turbulence (CNBC).

2) European sovereign flashpoints: French government debt is under acute pressure, reviving euro-area contagion fears.
   - French 10-year yield hit 5% for the first time since 2002 (FT/Reuters).
   - French debt risk premium reached “euro zone sovereign crisis” levels (Reuters).

3) AI boom, funding and painful build‑out costs: enormous capital flows into AI are colliding with steep operating and infrastructure costs, straining valuations and credit risk.
   - Anthropic reported quarterly revenue up nearly 50% to $16.8bn ahead of IPO (FT).
   - DeepSeek raised about $15bn in a Tencent/CATL-backed round (Reuters/Investing.com).
   - OpenAI nearing up to $30bn funding at ~$1.25tn valuation and selling top models on Google Cloud (CNBC/Reuters).
   - Reports of AI build‑out hitting corporate cashflows and sparking a surge in default-insurance trading (FT/Reuters).

4) Data‑center and AI infrastructure stress: hyperscaler capex, power/water constraints and financing strain are creating a bottleneck and losers among specialist suppliers.
   - CBRE moves $1bn deeper into data centers (WSJ).
   - Carrier planning a $600m Texas plant to make cooling gear for AI data centers (WSJ).
   - CoreWeave confidence eroding as bond yields spike and its stock/sentiment slumped (MarketWatch/CNBC).
   - Asian data‑centre markets raising concerns over water and power availability (CNBC).

5) Geopolitical risk and energy shock: Middle East escalation and attacks have lifted oil sharply and driven near-term safe-haven flows and supply worries.
   - Brent topped $113/bbl; oil rose above $112 in multiple reports (CNBC/WSJ/Reuters).
   - US ordered some personnel/diplomats to leave Saudi Arabia amid Houthi/region tensions (Reuters/CNBC).
   - Middle‑East oil exports said to have climbed back to pre‑war levels while risks remain (FT/Reuters).

6) Tech‑geo and platform governance crunch: Starlink/SpaceX, AI agents and big‑tech spending are provoking regulatory, security and political blowback.
   - Musk ordered Starlink slowdowns that blindsided Ukraine (WSJ); US lawmakers and Trump engaged on the issue.
   - US banks and Citi restricted/flagged unauthorized AI agents accessing accounts (FT/Reuters).
   - Meta faces a $25bn penalty (New Mexico case) and its credit‑risk indicator hit a record high as AI spending pressures cash flow (Reuters/CNBC).

7) Risk‑off rotation across equities and credit: AI‑led sentiment swings, oil shock and sovereign stress pushed stocks lower and boosted demand for high‑quality bonds.
   - Asian stocks hit 3‑month lows; China CSI 300 extended a 10‑week rout and entered bear territory (CNBC/FT).
   - AI stocks slid after disappointing earnings; markets opened lower amid renewed AI jitters (WSJ/Reuters).
   - Headlines noted investors moving into high‑quality bonds and safe havens; mortgage demand and housing showed warning signs as Fed considerations continue (CNBC).

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑longer interest‑rate regime complicated by an expensive, capital‑intensive AI build‑out and rising geopolitical risk that is lifting energy prices. That mix is producing episodic risk‑off moves: sovereign and credit stress in parts of Europe, heavy volatility in AI/data‑centre names, and recurrent flight‑to‑quality into high‑grade bonds.
