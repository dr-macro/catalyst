- US GDP growth: 3.4% annualised in Q3 (beat forecasts)

- Treasury / fiscal strains and private fund liquidity moves (private equity fund withdrawal limits cited)

Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a late‑cycle, higher‑rate regime where central banks remain tilted hawkish, bond yields and credit spreads are more reactive, and equity markets are being repriced for weaker AI profitability and higher capex risk; geopolitical shocks and large cyber incidents are adding episodic risk premia. Expect persistent volatility across rates, tech, energy and credit as markets reassess growth, policy and tail risks.
