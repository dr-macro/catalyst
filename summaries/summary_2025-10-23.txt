  - EU’s 19th Russia package includes LNG ban

  - US 10yr yields gap to fair value narrowing
  - Treasuries pushed down ahead of CPI

  - Shutdown reached 2nd longest on record

  - OPEC says ready to raise output if needed

8) Market structure is being reshaped by retail/meme flows, quantum/AI speculation, and episodic liquidity moves.
  - Beyond Meat short interest surged past 100% of public float
  - Quantum and small-cap quantum-related stocks jumped after reports of potential US government stake interest
  - Gold experienced extreme moves (see next)

9) Safe‑haven assets are volatile as markets digest sanctions and inflation signals; gold has seen outsized swings.
  - Gold plunged ~5% in one of the largest single-day drops since 2020
  - COMEX gold settlements noted large intraday moves; some strategists projecting higher longer‑term targets

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a market regime driven by geopolitics and policy uncertainty: sanctions and trade tensions are creating commodity-driven volatility while inflation data and Fed expectations keep rates and risk assets tightly coupled to headline prints. Equity gains remain narrow and tech/AI‑led, credit and private‑capital flows are active, and fiscal risks (shutdown) add a meaningful downside tail to growth.
