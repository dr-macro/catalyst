Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in an earnings‑led equity advance powered by an AI/data‑centre capex boom, but that same theme has increased market fragility as long yields have repriced higher and liquidity/credit strains surface. Geopolitical risk (Middle East, Ukraine) is amplifying commodity volatility and prompting unprecedented FX intervention, leaving markets vulnerable to swift regime shifts if policy, supply or AI risk narratives change.
