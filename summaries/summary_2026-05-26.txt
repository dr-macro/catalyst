Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a narrow, AI‑led equity rally running against a broader repricing of risk: higher real yields and liquidity strains are forcing central banks to stay hawkish even as geopolitical shocks (Middle East, Russia) drive commodity volatility and inflation upside. That combination raises stagflation and volatility risk — equities remain resilient but concentration and event risk have materially increased.
