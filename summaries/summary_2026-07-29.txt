8) Commodities and supply‑side shocks beyond oil are adding to macro uncertainty.  
   - U.S. oil inventories described as “precariously low.”  
   - Chile storm disrupted copper mines, raising AI supply concerns.  
   - Reports of rare‑earth stockpiles and Russia extending diesel export restrictions; OPEC+ likely to pause output hikes after September.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
We are in a higher‑volatility, risk‑off regime driven by geopolitical escalation (Middle East) that is lifting oil and commodity prices while pushing bond yields higher; central banks remain ambiguous, leaving markets to price tighter policy. Simultaneously, an overstretched AI/capex cycle is producing credit and equity stress in tech and chip markets, amplifying regional market routs and funding‑cost volatility.
