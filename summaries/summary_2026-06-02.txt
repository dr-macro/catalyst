Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a “growth‑led but fragile” regime: equity markets are driven by an AI investment boom and concentrated tech leadership, while higher real yields, liquidity strain from heavy Treasury borrowing and an energy supply shock from Middle East tensions are materially increasing downside risk and volatility. Policy and legal frictions (export controls, state lawsuits, central‑bank credibility concerns) are raising structural uncertainty for risk assets.
