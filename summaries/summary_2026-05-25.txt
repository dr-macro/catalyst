1) Energy shock amplified by Middle East conflict is squeezing supply and raising prices.
   - Oil down ~5%; Brent fell below $100/barrel.
   - Fertiliser groups cutting production due to sulphur shortages.
   - Coking coal prices surged after China’s worst mine disaster in 17 years.
   - US oil producers increasing output to capture war-driven price surge.

2) U.S.–Iran talks and a possible reopening of the Strait of Hormuz are driving rapid risk-asset moves.
   - Mediators discussing a 60‑day ceasefire extension.
   - Iran reportedly would open Hormuz ~30 days after a peace deal.
   - Vessels carrying Middle East oil/LNG exiting Hormuz toward Pakistan/China.
   - Dollar slid and stocks rallied on deal signs; gold climbed.

3) The bond market is under stress: yields and real rates have moved sharply and liquidity is tightening.
   - Real yields near 20‑year highs.
   - Treasury borrowing accelerates and liquidity conditions beginning to tighten.
   - Mortgage rates hit a nine‑month high.
   - Fed minutes show readiness to hike further if inflation persists.

4) An AI‑driven tech boom is concentrating market attention and fueling a wave of mega‑IPOs and corporate moves.
   - SpaceX, OpenAI and Anthropic IPOs flagged as imminent; SpaceX aiming at the largest IPO in history.
   - Anthropic expected to hit its first profitable quarter.
   - OpenAI preparing to file for an IPO very soon.
   - AI compute cost for one Cannes film reported at $400,000.

5) Tech governance, national security and industrial policy are fragmenting global supply chains and regulation.
   - EU planning measures to force purchases from non‑Chinese suppliers.
   - Euro stablecoin pilot backed by 37 banks; ECB rebuffed proposals as too risky.
   - France announced €1.5bn for quantum chips/microchips; US to award $2bn to quantum firms.
   - China banned Nvidia’s gaming chip during Jensen Huang’s visit; rare‑earth tensions with Japan resurfacing.

6) Geopolitical volatility remains elevated beyond Iran, pressuring markets and commodity flows.
   - Israel seized ~1,000 sq km as part of its war strategy.
   - Russia employed hypersonic (Oreshnik) missile strikes on Ukraine in heavy attacks.
   - Major coal‑mine disaster in China killed dozens (reports of ~90+ initially).
   - Hundreds to thousands of suspected Ebola cases reported in DRC; WHO warns response struggling.

7) Market sentiment is bifurcated: risk assets and tech bets hit new highs even as debt markets and funding stress raise systemic concerns.
   - Hedge funds boosting tech exposures to near‑record highs (Goldman Sachs note).
   - Short sellers made >$2.3bn betting against gambling companies.
   - Delivery Hero takeover activity: initial €10bn bid; later reports of an €11.5bn approach.
   - Stocks rallied on Iran peace hopes while Treasury rout tested Washington’s tolerance for higher borrowing costs.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a risk-on environment driven by AI momentum and episodic geopolitical détente (Iran talks), but it sits atop a fragile foundation: elevated real yields, tightening Treasury liquidity, and an energy shock that keeps inflation and fiscal costs exposed. Policy fragmentation and concentrated tech flows mean volatility can spike quickly between equity exuberance and fixed‑income repricing.
