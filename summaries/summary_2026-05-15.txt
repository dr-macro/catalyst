Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a supply‑shock + reflation regime: geopolitically driven energy shortages are colliding with sticky inflation, sending real yields higher and provoking a broad bond rout while equities remain narrowly led by AI winners. Policy is turning hawkish (leadership change at the Fed, markets pricing hikes), volatility is elevated, and positioning should prioritize energy/defense exposures, duration risk management, and dispersion within tech.
