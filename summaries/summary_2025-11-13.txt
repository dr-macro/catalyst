Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a two‑speed, liquidity‑sensitive market: risk assets are bid on an AI/data‑centre investment boom and fiscal certainty from the shutdown fix, while policy uncertainty (Fed cut odds), rising yields, and growing credit/housing stress keep volatility and dispersion high. Positioning should favor idiosyncratic AI beneficiaries and selective cyclicals, with hedges for policy/data shocks and downside risks in credit and consumer‑exposed sectors.
