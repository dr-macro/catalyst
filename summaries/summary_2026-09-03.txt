Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a higher‑rates, higher‑risk‑premium regime driven by an energy‑supply shock and geopolitical escalation that’s repricing sovereign and corporate borrowing costs. Simultaneously, rapid AI adoption is concentrating profits and capex into a narrow group of winners (and data‑centre assets), creating cross‑asset volatility as central banks and policymakers attempt to reconcile growth, inflation and security risks.
