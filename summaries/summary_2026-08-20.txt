Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a high‑geopolitical‑risk, policy‑active regime where bond markets are the flashpoint: sovereign debt growth and long‑end yields have forced unprecedented Treasury buybacks and active intervention, producing intermittent liquidity dislocations. Simultaneously, an accelerating AI/data‑centre capex cycle and Middle East supply risks are driving sectoral divergences (semis, energy, defence) and episodic safe‑haven flows into gold and hard assets.
