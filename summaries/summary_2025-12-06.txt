Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a growth‑skewed, policy‑uncertain regime: risk assets are buoyed by AI narratives and deal activity while bond markets are repricing Fed timing, pushing long rates and credit costs higher. That mix raises volatility and tail risk — large corporate debt loads and regulatory friction create asymmetric downside should growth or liquidity signals weaken.
