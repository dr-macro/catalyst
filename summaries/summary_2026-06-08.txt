Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a high-rate, high-geopolitical‑risk environment where sticky labour/inflation data has pushed Fed easing further out, tightening liquidity and lifting real yields; concurrently an AI‑driven capital reallocation is creating extreme concentration and volatility in tech and chip markets while an energy shock from Middle East hostilities raises inflation and supply‑risk concerns. Markets are navigating competing forces of monetary tightening, fiscal/defence spending and rapid tech capitalization, increasing tail‑risk for both equities and credit.
