Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
We are in a “higher‑for‑longer”-rate, high‑volatility regime driven by sticky inflation, large fiscal deficits and active sovereign interventions that are colliding with rapid, concentrated private capital flows into AI and semiconductors. Overlaying that are acute geopolitical energy and trade shocks, producing outsized dispersion between winners (AI/cloud, cyber) and losers (energy‑exposed consumers, parts of manufacturing and agriculture).
