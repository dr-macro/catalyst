Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
Geopolitical risk (US‑Iran war + Houthi blockade) is the dominant market driver, lifting energy prices, defense spending and safe‑haven flows while creating inflation upside surprises. At the same time an AI/chip investment boom and high‑profile tech corporate activity are concentrating equity risk—producing volatile rotations, rising real yields and frequent policy interventions (tariffs, export controls, market support). Overall: risk‑on pockets in tech/AI coexist with cyclical energy/defence rallies and higher‑for‑longer rate risk that keeps fixed income and real assets sensitive to shocks.
