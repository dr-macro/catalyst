Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We are in a regime of elevated geopolitical risk (Middle East + Ukraine) that is driving commodity volatility and risk premia, while central‑bank policy ambiguity and heavy Treasury issuance push real yields higher and steepen curves. Simultaneously, an AI‑led tech capex cycle is concentrating gains and volatility in semiconductors and related equities, creating a sharp risk‑on / risk‑off bifurcation across asset classes.
