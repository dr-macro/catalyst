Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a disinflationary but fragile regime — headline inflation has fallen to ~3%, tilting markets toward priced‑in easing, which has fueled a risk‑on rally led by tech/AI. At the same time, real‑economy bifurcation, corporate cost cuts, trade/policy shocks and geopolitical sanctions create elevated tail risks, so positioning needs to balance cyclical risk‑taking with defense against policy‑driven volatility.
