Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a regime of geopolitically driven commodity and risk‑premia volatility overlaid on an AI‑led tech investment boom that is concentrating earnings and capex in semiconductors — producing large sectoral dispersion. Inflation is cooling but central banks remain cautious, keeping real yields elevated and markets sensitive to liquidity and headline shocks (IPOs, lockups, military escalations).
