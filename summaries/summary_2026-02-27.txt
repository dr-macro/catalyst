Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in an AI‑driven, capital‑intensive growth cycle punctuated by high policy and national‑security friction: massive private funding and corporate capex compete with regulatory standoffs and tech re‑rating. At the same time elevated geopolitical risk (Middle East, Ukraine, Venezuela) is supporting commodity and safe‑haven bids, while bond markets are volatile but still pricing a later easing cycle—creating a fragile, risk‑sensitive backdrop for equities and credit.
