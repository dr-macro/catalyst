Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We’re in a narrow, AI‑led risk‑on market where concentrated tech dealmaking and chip news are driving equity indices higher, while political shocks (shutdowns, tariffs) and regulatory headlines inject episodic volatility. Simultaneously, markets are pricing eventual Fed easing—supporting bonds, gold and crypto—so risk assets are sensitive to policy cues and headline geopolitical/trade developments.
