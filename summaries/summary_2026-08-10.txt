Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a risk-on, AI-driven equity rally backed by strong tech capital flows and improving earnings expectations, but it is punctuated by rising geopolitical and supply risks (Hormuz, regional defence pacts) and active policy interference (FX interventions, political pressure on central banks). That mix is keeping longer-term yields and commodity prices volatile and elevating event-driven tail risk for macro asset allocators.
