Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
We are in a risk-on/risk-off environment driven by an AI-led tech boom that is already generating real economic (power, capex) and corporate-finance effects, while surging geopolitical risk (US–Iran strikes, Hormuz) is lifting oil, the dollar and safe-haven flows. Central banks are split and markets are repricing shorter-term rates and real yields, producing sharp sector rotations (chips, mega-caps) and elevated volatility across equities, credit and EM assets.
