Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a higher‑for‑longer interest‑rate regime with elevated real yields, driven by inflationary risks (notably energy) and a hawkish central‑bank stance; equities show concentrated AI/IPO‑led strength but are exposed to bond‑market and liquidity repricing. Geopolitical shocks (Iran/Strait of Hormuz, Russia‑Ukraine) and accelerated tech‑security decoupling raise downside tail risk and market dispersion.
