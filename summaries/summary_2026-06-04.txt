1) Inflation and bond-market repricing are back in focus as real yields climb and central banks look poised to tighten further.  
   - Real yields near 20-year highs.  
   - Eurozone inflation 3.2% in May.  
   - Treasury borrowing accelerating; liquidity conditions beginning to tighten.

2) An AI-driven capital cycle is concentrating market gains: massive equity raises, IPOs and capex are financing rapid expansion in data‑centres and AI infrastructure.  
   - Alphabet equity raise upsized to about $84.75–85bn (multiple reports).  
   - SpaceX IPO targeting ~$75bn raise at $135 per share and implied valuation ~$1.75–1.8tn.  
   - Anthropic files for IPO and expanding Mythos access to >15 countries / offering EU access.  
   - HPE shares +37% on AI infrastructure demand; LG to adopt 10,000 Nvidia GPUs.

3) Middle East hostilities and energy‑supply moves are driving an energy shock that pressures inflation and growth outlooks.  
   - U.S. crude stocks at lowest levels since 2004.  
   - OECD warns of a severe global slowdown if Gulf energy crisis drags on.  
   - Adnoc planning a UAE pipeline to bypass the Strait of Hormuz; Trafigura warns oil at an “inflection point.”

4) Liquidity stress is surfacing in private credit and alternatives as redemptions spike and managers impose limits.  
   - Cliffwater redemption requests hit 17% in its flagship private credit fund.  
   - Blackstone caps withdrawals from its flagship private credit fund; Partners Group limiting withdrawals.

5) Trade, industrial policy and tech sovereignty measures are increasing policy uncertainty for supply chains and investors.  
   - U.S. proposes tariffs of at least 10% after forced‑labour probe.  
   - EU to broaden import quotas and tariffs against China.  
   - India to cut capital gains tax for foreign investors in government bonds (policy change reported).

6) Labour and real‑income dynamics are weakening beneath the surface despite continued hiring, raising demand and consumption risks.  
   - Real wages starting to shrink in developed countries.  
   - 1 in 4 white‑collar workers report no raise/no promotion.  
   - ADP private payrolls +122,000 in May; weekly U.S. jobless claims ticked up.

7) Market concentration and sector rotation are producing single‑stock volatility as some high‑expectation tech names disappoint.  
   - Broadcom revenue/forecast miss sparked a sharp selloff and warnings of a multibillion‑dollar market‑cap hit (~$300bn cited).  
   - Leveraged ETF assets doubled in two months as investors pressed AI bets.  
   - Vanguard ETF became first ETF to top $1tn in assets.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):
We’re in a higher‑for‑longer rates environment driven by sticky inflation and tighter liquidity, coinciding with an AI‑led equity rally concentrated in a few megacaps and supported by outsized capital raises and IPOs. That confluence — rising yields, energy/ geopolitical risk and redemption stress in private credit — creates pronounced cross‑asset dispersion and heightened event risk for macro strategies.
