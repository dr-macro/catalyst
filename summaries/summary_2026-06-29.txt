Macro/markets regime (2–3 lines)
We are in a late‑cycle, policy‑sensitive market where an AI‑led growth narrative is financing a leveraged risk rally even as real yields rise and central banks/authorities tighten governance of frontier tech. Simultaneously elevated geopolitical and climate tail risks (Middle East, Russia‑Ukraine, heatwaves, energy stocks) are creating episodic commodity volatility and driving a cautious bid for bonds and real assets despite continued tech‑sector momentum.
