1) AI boom is driving massive capital flows, concentration and system risk.  
   - Tencent leases 100,000 chips from Oracle.  
   - Wall Street banks arranged a record $60bn chip financing for Broadcom and Anthropic.  
   - SpaceX seeks ~$40bn financing to buy Nvidia GPUs.  
   - Nvidia licensing deal with Groq cited at $20bn (facing lawsuit).  
   - Global pension funds cutting US equities over AI concentration risk.  
   - Anthropic expanding models and preparing IPO; Mistral and other new models launched.

2) Global government bond yields have surged to multi-decade highs, straining borrowers and markets.  
   - US 10-year yield hit a 24‑year high.  
   - 30‑year US Treasury reached its highest since 2002.  
   - UK 30‑year gilt hit a 28‑year high.  
   - Biggest quarterly surge in yields since 1994 noted; mortgage rates near a ~3‑year high.  
   - Corporate borrowing costs and commercial real‑estate deals under pressure.

3) Middle East conflict and Houthi attacks are tightening oil/fuel markets and shipping, raising price and logistics risk.  
   - Saudi Aramco warns global oil stockpiles are “scarily thin.”  
   - Attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz hit the highest weekly level since the start of the war.  
   - Supertanker charter to China cost ~$76m (10x pre‑war levels).  
   - Captains being paid ~$100,000/month to transit Hormuz.  
   - IEA/IEA members discussing diesel/releases; Shell and refiners reporting record margins.

4) Geopolitical escalation (Russia, Ukraine, Iran/Houthis) is intensifying security and sanction risk.  
   - Russia made threats over Kaliningrad and reportedly told commanders to abandon rules of war.  
   - Russian strikes on Kyiv and probes into a plague‑lab death ongoing.  
   - Houthis attacked Saudi airports and Aden; Iran-related threats to shipping routes reported.  
   - EU set to approve new Russia sanctions; NATO tensions and troop movements reported.

5) Market divergence: AI-led tech stocks push equity indices to records even as macro risks mount.  
   - S&P 500 and Nasdaq reached record closing highs led by AI names.  
   - Later sessions saw S&P/Nasdaq retreat as yields and oil climbed.  
   - “Magnificent Seven”/AI leaders concentrated gains amid broader market stress.

6) Large leveraged deals and high corporate debt loads are drawing scrutiny amid higher rates.  
   - Paramount/Warner deal financing cited at ~$110–111bn; debt risk flagged.  
   - Schneider Electric agreed to buy PTC for $23.7bn.  
   - Multiple megadeals and LBOs (and Skydance/Paramount combinations) spotlighted for debt exposure.  
   - SpaceX and other firms’ aggressive chip borrowing plans raising credit‑risk concerns.

7) Central banks remain cautious-to‑hawkish and fiscal vulnerabilities are increasingly visible.  
   - Fed minutes show division but signal another hike may be coming.  
   - India raised rates for the first time since 2023.  
   - BOJ may signal underlying inflation at 2%.  
   - French central bank warns the country risks being “strangled by interest rates”; IMF urges fiscal restraint.

8) Cybersecurity, legal and regulatory risk from AI and data breaches is rising across sectors.  
   - OpenAI/Altman faces legal exposure after dozens of hacks were uncovered.  
   - AI models were used in bank cyberattacks (South Korea).  
   - Goldman Sachs and Man Group exposed in an EY data breach.  
   - Regulators and governments debating new AI rules, bans on devices (e.g., AI glasses) and platform oversight.

9) Europe is experiencing political and social strain that amplifies fiscal and market fragility.  
   - French student protests described as the heaviest toll in decades; strikes spreading in Europe.  
   - Spain called a snap election; French bond market turmoil prompted consideration of short‑term debt issuance.  
   - Hungary set to drop vetoes on EU bids; political shifts (Le Pen, AfD regional gains) heighten policy uncertainty.

Current macro/markets regime (2–3 lines):  
A classic risk‑off undercurrent driven by a global bond sell‑off and energy‑security shock is colliding with a narrowly concentrated AI‑led equity rally — producing high market dispersion, stressed credit conditions for debt‑heavy corporates and elevated geopolitical tail risks. Policy remains data‑dependent but tilted toward higher rates, leaving fiscal and liquidity vulnerabilities exposed.
